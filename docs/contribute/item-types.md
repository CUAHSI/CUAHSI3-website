# What you can add to the website, in plain words

For CUAHSI staff. Each section says what the item is, where it shows on the site, the facts it needs and
an example. If you are asking for an item (level 1, [ask.md](ask.md)), put the facts in the list into the
request form. An agent writes the file; you never need to read it.

Two rules apply to every item:

- **Say where the facts came from.** For an item about a thing, link the thing itself: the event's
  sign-up page, the paper's DOI, the tool's site. For a summary of an article, link the article. If the
  facts came from you directly, say so; that counts as the source. A welcome or a staff announcement needs no link.
- **Nothing is guessed.** If you do not know a date, a link or a name, say it is unknown. The item can wait
  unpublished until it is known.

Every address below is on the website. For example, `/community/events` is
https://cuahsi3-website.netlify.app/community/events, the demo site, which search engines do not list.

## Which one is it?

| You have | It is | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Something happening on a date: a workshop, webinar, deadline, conference session | an **event** | It has a start, and it moves to the past once it starts |
| An announcement that matters now and fades: a new staff member, a service change, a call that closes, a launch | a **news item** | Short-lived; nobody needs it in a year |
| An outcome worth reading a year from now: what a program achieved, research CUAHSI enabled, a tool's impact | an **impact story** (staff often say "highlight") | Lasting; it is what CUAHSI reports to funders |
| A change to your own team entry or profile page | a **profile update** | |

If you are not sure between news and an impact story, say so. The agent proposes one and says why, and a
website owner decides.

## Event

**What it is.** Something on a date that people can attend or act on: a workshop, field course, webinar,
webinar series, conference session or deadline.

**Where it shows.**
- On its own page at `/community/events/<name>`.
- In the list at `/community/events`; once it has started, under "Past", which keeps the 12 most recent.
- On the home page (the next 3 upcoming events) and on `/community` (the next 4), until it starts.
- Workshops and field courses also show on the Learn & Train archive, `/learn-train/archive`.

**Facts it needs.**
- Title.
- Type: workshop, field course, webinar, webinar series, conference or deadline.
- Who it is for: for example students, faculty, community.
- Start date and time, end date and time, and the time zone.
- Where it happens:
  - online, in person or both;
  - the city, if in person;
  - its web page. This is kept with the event, but the event's page does not show it yet. Give a
    sign-up link as well if there is one.
- A short description in plain text, two to four sentences. It cannot hold a link: the page shows it as
  plain text.
- Registration: whether it is needed, the sign-up link, the cost. The sign-up link shows as a "Register"
  button until the event starts.
- Optional: a few topic words, such as `HydroShare` or `snow`; whether the newsletter announced it.
- Optional: one picture, either a photo or a single graphic such as a flyer or slide, with what it shows,
  and a caption and a credit if there are any. It shows on the event's own page, not in the lists.

**Example.** [CUAHSI Turns 25: Shaping the Future of Water Science Infrastructure and Education](https://cuahsi3-website.netlify.app/community/events/cuahsi-turns-25-town-hall-2026),
CUAHSI's town hall at AGU 2026: a conference session, in person in San Francisco, 9 December 2026, 1 to 2 pm Pacific.

## News item

**What it is.** A short announcement that matters now: a new staff member, a board election, a launch, a
service change or incident, a call for applications.

**Where it shows.**
- On its own page at `/community/news/<name>`.
- In the list at `/community/news`.
- Among the 3 latest news items on `/community`.

**Facts it needs.**
- Title.
- The date of the announcement.
- One or two sentences for the summary that shows in lists.
- The text: a few short paragraphs. If it is about an outside article, summarise it in your own words and
  link it; do not copy it.
- The link to the thing it is about, if there is one.
- Optional: a picture, placed in the text, with a description of what it shows. A caption or credit can
  only be a line of text under it.
- Optional: a few topic words.

**Example.** [The CUAHSI Water Learning Commons](https://cuahsi3-website.netlify.app/community/news/water-learning-commons-launch):
the date, a one-sentence summary, three paragraphs, a link to the Commons.

## Impact story (a "highlight")

**What it is.** An outcome worth reading a year from now. It might be what a program or workshop produced,
research that CUAHSI's data or tools made possible, or a community achievement. The site's address and
menu call these impact stories. The list page and profile pages still say "highlights", as staff often do,
and old `/highlights/` addresses forward to them.

**Where it shows.**
- On its own page at `/about/impact/<name>`.
- In the list at `/about/impact`, filtered by year and category.
- The newest story is the large feature on the home page.
- On the Data & Computing page, `/data-platforms`, next to a tool the story is tagged with (up to 2 stories
  per tool). Today only HydroShare's tag matches any story.
- On the profile page of each staff member it names, if they have a profile page (up to 4 stories each).

**Facts it needs.**
- Title.
- The date of the outcome, or of the story.
- Category: research, cyberinfrastructure, data infrastructure, training or community. Suggest one; a website
  owner decides.
- One or two sentences for the summary that shows in lists.
- The text: what happened, who took part, what came of it, in your own words.
- The link to the thing itself: the modules, the paper, the dataset, the tool.
- Which CUAHSI staff it names.
- Partners, and funding: the funder and the award number, so the story can carry the funder's
  acknowledgment.
- Optional: one picture, with what it shows, a caption and a credit.

**Example.** [HydroLearn–CIROH Hackathon Produces 11 New Learning Modules](https://cuahsi3-website.netlify.app/about/impact/hydrolearn-ciroh-hackathon-2026):
category training, partners CIROH and HydroLearn, NOAA funding with its award number.

## Profile update

**What it is.** A change to your own entry on the team page, `/about/team`, or to your profile page. You
can ask to change your own entry only.

**Where it shows.**
- **On the team page,** your card shows your photo (or your initials), name, pronouns and role.
- **On your profile page,** `/about/team/<your-name>`, everything else shows:
  - your bio (two to four sentences);
  - a fun fact;
  - links to ORCID, Google Scholar, GitHub and LinkedIn;
  - optional longer sections under the bio, for example Career, Education and Selected publications.
    [Jordan Read's profile](https://cuahsi3-website.netlify.app/about/team/jordan-read) is an example;
  - lists added for you: up to 4 impact stories and 4 newsletter issues that name you, and cyberseminars
    you spoke in.

**Only some people have a profile page.** 6 of the 22 people on the team page have one; for everyone else
the page does not exist yet. Your bio, fun fact and links are kept, but they show nowhere until it is turned
on. Whether yours is turned on is a website owner's decision: ask in your request.

**Facts it needs.** The new text or links, exactly as you want them; for a photo, the image file. Leave out
personal contact details such as a phone number or a home address.

## Handled by someone else, for now

| Item | Who handles it |
|---|---|
| Jobs | An automated agent collects water jobs, and a website owner reviews what it finds. To suggest a job, send its link with the request form. |
| Adding a new staff member, or removing one | A website owner. |
| Newsletter issues | Added by a website owner after each issue is sent, with its events, news and stories. |
| Cyberseminar recordings | A website owner, from the YouTube recording. |
| Critical Zone papers and datasets | Staff: the request form, with the title, a DOI or link, and the award or observatory. People outside CUAHSI email help@cuahsi.org, as the Critical Zone pages say. |
| Programs, data portals, documents, board and member pages | Ask a website owner. |
| Anything else: a new page, a layout change, a web address | Level 3 in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). |
