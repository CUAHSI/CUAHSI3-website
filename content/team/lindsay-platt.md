## Recent work at CUAHSI

- Builds the automated data processing, reporting and visualizations behind CUAHSI's business operations and interdisciplinary research. Supervisor since April 2025; acting Lead of Operations from June 2025 to November 2026.

- Teaches open and reproducible water science at conferences. At AGU24 (Washington, DC) she presented *Technical Skills for Publishing and Sharing Data and Code* with Kristina Vrouwenvelder, and was an invited speaker with *Practical Tips and Concepts to Start Your Reproducibility Journey*, with Abner Bogan, Clara Cogswell and Jordan Read. The same group presented *Bridging the reproducibility gap* at WaterSciCon24 (St. Paul, MN).

- Introduced the R `targets` package for building reproducible data pipelines, with Abner Bogan at the ESIP January Meeting 2025 and with Elise Hinman at the 2025 National Monitoring Conference (Green Bay, WI), where she also presented *Coding strategies for building robust and reproducible water data analyses*.

- Spoke on better data and code practice at the 2025 ASLO Aquatic Sciences Meeting (Charlotte, NC) and the Hacking Limnology 2025 Data Science and Open Science Summit, and on CUAHSI's free, open-source data tools with Clara Cogswell at the 2025 Universities Council on Water Resources conference (Minneapolis, MN).

- Invited seminar speaker in Virginia Tech's Biological Systems Engineering seminar series, April 2025: *Perspectives from a data-driven career (so far)*.

## Career

- **Environmental Data Scientist, CUAHSI**, October 2023 to present. Applies data science to CUAHSI's operations and research through automated data processing, reporting and visualizations.
- **Graduate research assistant, Center for Limnology** (part-time, Madison, Wisconsin), August 2022 to August 2024. Modeled sediment dynamics in Lake Superior from meteorological, hydrological and remotely sensed data.
- **Water Data Scientist, U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)**, July 2015 to September 2023. Built reproducible data workflows and web visualizations, and led a national scientific computing training program, including an Introduction to R course that has taught over 630 people (her count). Was the main voice behind the @USGS_DataSci Twitter account, 2015 to 2020.
- **Undergraduate analyst, Virginia Department of Environmental Quality** (at Virginia Tech), April 2013 to May 2015. Hydro-statistical analysis of water supply impact and availability.

## Education

- **B.S. Biological Systems Engineering** (watershed engineering), Virginia Tech, 2015.
