  The first Water Science Exchange, a two-and-a-half day gathering at the
  University of Missouri that brings together water science researchers across
  career stages and institutions for skill-building workshops, a poster

  October 29. Poster proposals are due October 21; posters are shown to up to
  100 attendees. A special topics forum on AI in the water sciences is led by
  CUAHSI CEO Jordan Read. Field trips: the Central Mississippi River Basin
  Long-Term Agroecosystem Research (LTAR) site tour (USDA-ARS) and the
  Missouri Ozarks AmeriFlux (MOFLUX) site tour (University of Missouri and Oak
  Ridge National Laboratory). A limited number of travel stipends are
  available, first come, first served, with preference for students and
  postdocs presenting a poster; request one when registering. The hotel room
  block deadline is October 15.
