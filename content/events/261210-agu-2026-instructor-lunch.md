description: >
  A one-hour lunch session at the AGU Annual Meeting
  (December 7 to 11, 2026) for instructors who teach water science, with CUAHSI
  staff, on two ways to support teaching: free HydroLearn modules, online lessons
  built and shared by faculty, and teaching a CUAHSI Virtual University (CVU)
  course to widen a department's course offerings without adding to the teaching
  load. Lunch is provided. Instructors who attend and complete a post-event survey
  are eligible for up to $500 in travel support, reimbursed after the event.
  Space and travel support are limited: sign up through the interest form with a
  short note on why you want to come. Submissions are reviewed on a rolling basis
  from November 23 and invitations are sent by December 2; later submissions are
  reviewed as timing and capacity allow. Priority goes to instructors new to
  HydroLearn or CVU and to instructors from institutions new to these programs.
  Questions, or interested but not going to AGU: Julia Masterman,
