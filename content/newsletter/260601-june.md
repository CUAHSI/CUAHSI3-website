## Announcements

### New staff

CUAHSI welcomes **Henok Tedla** as Controller. Henok is a finance and accounting professional with extensive experience leading financial management, budgeting, grant accounting, compliance, and financial reporting functions for nonprofit and mission-driven organizations.

### 25th anniversary: our founding member institutions

As part of CUAHSI's 25th anniversary, this issue looks back at the 33 universities that came together in 2001 to found the consortium. Representatives from all 33 founding institutions were elected to CUAHSI's first Board of Directors, shaping the organization's early governance and direction. In the 25 years since, membership has more than tripled as bylaws changes expanded eligibility to a wider range of organizations.

### Spring Virtual Open House — June 24

CUAHSI hosted its Spring Virtual Open House on June 24, 2026, 12:00–2:00 PM ET. Agenda highlights included CEO Jordan Read's welcome remarks, HydroShare development highlights from Martin Seul, a CIROH Community Hydrofabric Project overview from the research team, data skill-building training opportunities, an update on the Water Content Portal open learning platform, and engagement opportunities — followed by breakout discussions. CUAHSI hosts Virtual Open Houses twice a year; the next is planned for fall 2026.

### Community awards — nominations open

CUAHSI is accepting nominations for the **Education and Engagement Award** and the **Community Service Award**, recognizing meaningful contributions to hydrologic science education or the CUAHSI community. Nominations are open to the community at any career stage; self-nominations are accepted. Community Service Award nominees must be affiliated with a CUAHSI member institution. Nominations are due **August 31, 2026**.

### 2026 Near-Surface Geophysics Community Input Workshop

August 25–26, 2026, in Albuquerque, NM (in-person only). CUAHSI is collaborating with the NSF National Geophysical Facility (operated by EarthScope), CTEMPs, NHERI, OpenTopography, and the AGU Near-Surface Geophysics Section to bring together the community to assess gaps and produce a community-informed Workshop Report guiding future research and education investment. Travel support (hotel and domestic flight) is available for accepted attendees. Applications due **June 22, 2026**.

### A new open hydrology textbook — CUAHSI hosting

Backed by a VIVA Open Education grant, a team led by co-editors J.P. Gannon (Virginia Tech), Dom Ciruzzi (William & Mary), and Christa Kelleher (Lafayette College) is writing an openly licensed introductory hydrology textbook, with ten chapter authors and ten institutions already committed to adopting it. The project responds to a gap identified in a study of 43 introductory hydrology syllabi published in *Water Resources Research*. CUAHSI will host an interactive HTML version of the book and its source code at no charge, supported through CUAHSI's Open Learning initiatives. Writing begins summer 2026, with classroom piloting in spring 2027 and publication targeted for fall 2027.

### Water Data Forum: Water Management for Data Centers

Tuesday, June 30, 12:00–1:00 PM ET. Co-hosted with Cleveland Water Alliance and the Water Environment Federation, this panel discussion examined water and wastewater considerations in data center operations, with panelists from Park Place Technologies, PNNL, and Virginia Tech.

## CUAHSI's Data Publishing Program: Spring 2026 recap

The second Data Publishing Program cohort — *Get Your Data Across the Finish Line* — brought together 20 participants from 20 institutions across career stages, from graduate students to full professors and postdocs. Over six sessions across three weeks, participants built out HydroShare resources with data, metadata, and documentation, supported by CUAHSI staff, optional one-on-one coaching, and peer review. CUAHSI aims to run this program twice yearly; a Fall 2026 cohort is already in planning.

**Bridges to the Future Program** — $2,000 grants supporting undergraduate, master's, and doctoral students facing financial barriers to hydrology careers. Applications due October 15 to Hydrology Section President Venkataraman Lakshmi.

## Community engagement

CUAHSI staff connected with the community across several May conferences:

- **Abner Bogan** attended the 43rd annual Native American Fish and Wildlife Society National Conference (May 4–7, Verona, NY), CUAHSI's first time attending — an important opportunity to learn from practitioners working at the intersection of conservation, research, and Indigenous data governance.
- **Julia Masterman** attended the ASLO/SIL Joint Meeting (May 12–16, Montreal), hosting a CUAHSI exhibit booth and presenting a poster on supporting a thriving water sciences community.
- **Tony Castronova** co-presented a plenary talk on community-driven hydrofabric innovation for NOAA's NextGen Framework at the AWRA Geospatial Water Technology Conference (May 18–20, Niagara Falls), and attended the CSDMS Annual Meeting (May 21–22, Minneapolis).
- Staff from CUAHSI's research and software teams — Tony Castronova, Irene Garousi-Nejad, Danielle Tijerina-Kreuzer, Abner Bogan, Scott Black, and Callie Porter-Borden — attended the CIROH Developers Conference (May 27–29, Salt Lake City) and led three workshops on geospatial skills, the CIROH Community Hydrofabric, and co-developing FloodSavvy.
