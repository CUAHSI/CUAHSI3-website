## Announcements

### New staff

CUAHSI welcomes two new staff members:

**Danielle Tijerina-Kreuzer** joins as Research Scientist. Danielle specializes in physics-based hydrologic modeling, groundwater–surface water interactions, and high-performance computing workflows for large-scale hydrologic models.

**Barbara Walker** joins as Grants Administration Specialist. Barbara supports pre-award and post-award grant administration with a focus on compliance, reporting, subrecipient monitoring, and grant closeout.

### Apply to teach in the 2026 Fall CUAHSI Virtual University

Faculty: apply by **March 16, 2026**. The CUAHSI Virtual University (CVU) consists of self-contained 4-week modules taught by faculty at participating institutions. Students can enroll in up to three modules and earn credit through their home institution. Participating faculty teach one module, serve as instructor-of-record, and earn teaching credit. Contact Julia Masterman at jmasterman@cuahsi.org.

## News from the board

**Marco P. Maneta**, newly elected board member. Director of Flood Modeling at First Street, Inc. and Faculty Affiliate (formerly Professor of Hydrology) at the University of Montana Geosciences Department. Marco bridges academic hydrology and climate risk financial modeling, analyzing links between climate hazards and financial risk to support adaptation and risk mitigation decision-making.

## Voices from the community

**Punwath Prum**, PhD candidate, Department of Geology and Environmental Science, University of Pittsburgh. A CUAHSI HydroInformatics Innovation Fellowship (HIF) awardee, Punwath shares his research tracking global water quality changes in coastal rivers and estuaries using Landsat satellite data. The HIF enabled him to develop a harmonization algorithm and create the first global coastal surface reflectance database, which led to a NASA FINESST24 award and opportunities to present at AGU and ASLO. The dataset was added to HydroShare and will be made publicly accessible after peer review.

## Program engagement: Snow Measurement Field School

CUAHSI Science Education and Outreach Specialist Julia Masterman facilitated the **2026 Snow Measurement Field School**, January 5–9 at the YMCA of the Rockies, Snow Mountain Ranch in Granby, CO. Twenty-five students and seven instructors participated. The curriculum progressed from basic snow pit digging and measurements on day one to student-designed mini field campaigns by week's end. Highlights included visiting a SNOTEL site and Fraser Experimental Forest.

## HydroShare spotlight

This month's featured resource: **HIVIS Hydro-Haze Dataset: Diffusion-Leveraged GAN Dehazing Driven by Classification** by Moheb Henein, Mahmoud Ayyad, and Marouane Temimi. The dataset presents ground-based USGS streamgage camera images from nine Alaska stations, documenting image quality issues from harsh environmental conditions. Highlighted as a strong example of transparent documentation, quality categorization, and public accessibility supporting reuse and reproducibility.
