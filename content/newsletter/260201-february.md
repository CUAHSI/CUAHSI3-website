### Apply to be an instructor for the 2026 Fall semester of the CUAHSI Virtual University!

![CUAHSI Virtual University crest](/newsletter-images/2026-february/cuahsi-virtual-university-crest.png)

**Faculty**: apply [here](https://cuahsi.jotform.com/260414260402947) by March 16, 2026 to be an instructor for the CVU!

The CUAHSI Virtual University (CVU) is an educational program that enables participating faculty to offer short, specialized courses to a broader student audience. The program consists of 4-week modules taught by faculty in participating institutions. Modules are self-contained and students at participating institutions can enroll in up to three modules and obtain credit through their home institution. Participating faculty teach their module and serve as instructor-of-record for students from their own institution who enroll in CVU, enabling all students at their institution an opportunity to enroll.

The CVU is designed to:​

- expand instructional impact by increasing reach,
- expose students to a subdiscipline or specialized training that is not otherwise available,
- broaden the academic network of participants, and
- enhance the depth and breadth of graduate course offerings of participating institutions.

Faculty participation includes:

- teaching a one-credit self contained course module over 4 weeks,
- serving as the instructor-of-record for students at their institution, and.
- earning teaching credit through your home institution. ​

More details can be found on our [website](https://www.cuahsi.org/virtual-university).

If you have any questions, please contact Julia Masterman, [jmasterman@cuahsi.org](mailto:jmasterman@cuahsi.org).

### Applications Now Open: WaterSoftHack 2026

![WaterSoftHack logo](/newsletter-images/2026-february/watersofthack-logo.png)

July 20-31, 2026 (Virtual)

WaterSoftHack is a collaborative effort led by Dr. Vidya Samadi, Clemson University, with the University of Iowa, Tulane University and CUAHSI. The initiative aims to cultivate the workforce that creates, utilizes, and supports advanced cyberinfrastructure (CI) workflows and tools to enable and potentially transform fundamental water science education and research. This is a multi-year NSF-funded project, dedicated to advancing water science research and education through the synergistic power of CI and machine learning training.

This year will focus on cloud and edge computing, equipping the next generation of water science and engineering researchers with cutting-edge technology and cyberinfrastructure training. Participants will gain hands-on experience running machine learning models in cloud and edge computing environments, learning how to design scalable workflows, deploy models efficiently, and leverage modern computational resources for water research applications.

Graduate and undergraduate students, postdocs, engineers, professionals, and early-career faculty are encouraged to apply.

[Apply now through March 31, 2026](https://watersofthack.github.io)

## Voices From the Community

![Punwath Prum at a conference poster](/newsletter-images/2026-february/punwath-prum.jpg)

**Punwath Prum**, PhD candidate

Department of Geology and Environmental Science

University of Pittsburgh

Punwath Prun, a CUAHSI HydroInformatics Innovation Fellowship (HIF) awardee, shares his research on tracking global water quality changes in coastal rivers and estuaries using Landsat satellite data. The HIF enabled him to develop a harmonization algorithm and create the first global coastal surface reflectance database, which led to a NASA FINESST24 award and opportunities to present his findings at major conferences including AGU and ASLO. The dataset was added to [HydroShare](https://www.hydroshare.org/resource/ef728cff303a4d3bb49b69702283184c/) and will be made publicly accessible after the peer review process is complete.

## Program Engagement

### 2026 Snow Measurement Field School

CUAHSI Science Education and Outreach Specialist Julia Masterman facilitated the 2026 Snow Measurement Field School workshop, which brought together twenty-five students and seven instructors from January 5-9 at the YMCA of the Rockies, Snow Mountain Ranch in Granby, CO.

Participants gained fundamental training in methods for high-quality fieldwork taking snow measurements and gained greater context to remote sensing approaches. The curriculum progressed from basic snow pit digging and measurements on day one to student-designed mini field campaigns by week's end. Highlights included visiting a SNOTEL site and Fraser Experimental Forest. Students built valuable professional networks and learned from a fantastic group of instructors who brought their unique perspectives and areas of expertise to the training.

![Participants at the 2026 Snow Measurement Field School](/newsletter-images/2026-february/snow-field-school-2026.png)

## HydroShare

**HydroShare Data Spotlight:** Each month we highlight a published HydroShare resource that ranks within the top 10 most visited published resources over the past 30 days.

This month’s featured resource, [HIVIS Hydro-Haze Dataset: Diffusion-Leveraged GAN Dehazing Driven by Classification](https://www.hydroshare.org/resource/2aca7bf88a944b86af4cd4c4b01bed71/), by authors Moheb Henein, Mahmoud Ayyad, and Marouane Temimi present a dataset of ground-based USGS streamgage camera images from nine Alaska stations, documenting image quality issues caused by harsh environmental conditions. CUAHSI highlights this dataset as a strong example of adhering to data and metadata publishing best practices, demonstrating transparent documentation, quality categorization, and public accessibility that support reuse and reproducibility.
