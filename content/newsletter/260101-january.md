## Announcements

### CUAHSI at AGU 2025

CUAHSI participated in the American Geophysical Union (AGU) Annual Meeting, held December 15–19 in New Orleans, Louisiana. Nine CUAHSI staff attended the meeting, contributing to 18 presentations through panels, posters, and talks. The CUAHSI booth in the exhibit hall served as a central hub, with staff leading live demonstrations of HydroShare and a map where visitors marked their institutional facilities from around the world.

A highlight of the week was CUAHSI's community gathering on the evening of December 17 at Ernst Café, which brought **164 attendees** together for appetizers, games, and informal networking. Later that evening, 29 attendees joined the Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute Community Gathering to reconnect with alumni and engage with others working in water prediction.

### Final Call: 2026 Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute

Applications close **January 12, 2026** for graduate student Fellows for the 2026 Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute, June 8 – July 22, 2026, at the University of Alabama in Tuscaloosa, AL. 2026 marks the 10th Summer Institute hosted. Contact Julia Masterman at jmasterman@cuahsi.org with questions.

### HydroLearn-CIROH May/June 2026 Workshop and Hackathon

The HydroLearn team invites faculty, postdoctoral scholars, and graduate students with expertise in hydrology to apply by **January 31, 2026**. Event dates: in-person workshop May 27–29 at the CIROH Developers Conference (Salt Lake City, UT) and virtual hackathon June 2–5 and June 8–11, 2026. Selected participants receive travel support. Participants work in pairs to create a peer-reviewed HydroLearn module. Over 60 peer-reviewed modules are already on the platform.

## Voices from the community

**Masoumeh Hashemi**, Graduate Research Assistant, Plants, Soils & Climate Department, Utah State University. Her research focuses on improving water measurement in irrigation canals and ditches using low-cost, camera-based approaches powered by deep learning and image processing, supporting better decision-making for farmers, canal companies, and water managers in water-scarce regions.

## HydroShare spotlight

This month's featured resource supports the paper *An Analysis of Fixture Efficiency and Behavioral Factors of Indoor Residential Water Use of Single-Family Households*. The resource enables researchers to explore factors differentiating high and low water-using households, the relative importance of fixture efficiency versus behavior, and the effects of household size and weather across more than 33,000 households throughout the US.
