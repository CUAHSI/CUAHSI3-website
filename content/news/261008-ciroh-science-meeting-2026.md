Five CUAHSI staff attended the [Cooperative Institute for Research to Operations in Hydrology (CIROH)](https://ciroh.ua.edu/) Science Meeting in Tuscaloosa, Alabama, from September 14 to 17, 2026, and four of them presented on the following topics:

- **Jordan Read:** "Untapped and Undercounted: The Case for Coordinated CIROH Data Amplification" and "Hydrologic Process Synthesis to Inform NextGen Strategy"
- **Tony Castronova:** "Advancing NOAA's NextGen HydroFabric Through CIROH Collaboration"
- **Irene Garousi-Nejad:** "HydroShare in Practice: Accelerating FAIR-aligned CIROH Research Workflows"
- **Courtney Di Vittorio:** "Presenting New HydroLearn Modules and Planning for the Next Phase of CIROH"

Danielle Tijerina-Kreuzer also attended but did not present.

Covered in the [October 2026 e-newsletter](/community/newsletter/2026-october).
