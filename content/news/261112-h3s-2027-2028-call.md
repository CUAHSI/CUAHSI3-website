The [AGU Hydrology Section Student Subcommittee (H3S)](https://www.agu-h3s.org/) is accepting applications for 2027-2028 members. Members build their hydrology network, plan events for student and early career scientists, and organize AGU sessions and cyberseminars; H3S develops the Navigating Academic Waters cyberseminar series, which it hosts with CUAHSI. Past events and blog posts are on the H3S website.

Current members will be at the CUAHSI booth (#636) at the [AGU Annual Meeting](/community/events/agu-annual-meeting-2026) for anyone with questions. [Apply to join H3S](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScZIePW2g3VrxxM5AFNZ1tZUOgxNrtllGMtRKfZiLVD3Ntdqw/viewform).
