The latest reflection in the 25th anniversary series is from **JP Gannon**, chair of the CUAHSI Board of Directors.

Gannon has known CUAHSI since the start of his PhD. At first it was a way to meet other water scientists at AGU, and he remembers the CUAHSI mixer around 2011. Connections made at CUAHSI events led to some of his best and longest collaborations. As his career progressed, CUAHSI became a place to engage with colleagues on education through committee work, to store data in HydroShare, a source of funding to start a project, a partner in making free educational materials available, and a way to give back through service.

Nine months into his year as chair, he writes that serving on the board showed him how much work from many people it takes to keep an organization like CUAHSI running, and that it cannot work without community support. He asks readers to show that support so funders can see CUAHSI is critical to water science. His suggestions: join or renew your institution's membership, forward the newsletter or program announcements to colleagues, introduce new graduate students or faculty to CUAHSI, nominate yourself for the board or an advisory committee, nominate a colleague for an award, join a program, or host data in HydroShare.
