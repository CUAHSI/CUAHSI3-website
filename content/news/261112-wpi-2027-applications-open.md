Applications are open for the 2027 [Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute](/learn-train/programs/summer-institute), a seven-week training program for graduate students on operational water prediction. CUAHSI and the University of Alabama host it, with support from the Cooperative Institute for Research to Operations in Hydrology (CIROH). The 2027 program runs June 7 to July 21, 2027.

**Research Theme Leaders** set the big-picture research theme for student project teams and guide them through their projects; each supports or co-supports one to three teams of two to four students. Review begins on a rolling basis on December 1, 2026, and applications are accepted through December 16 or until enough have come in, so apply by December 1 for full consideration. [Theme Leader call and application](https://www.cuahsi.org/events/call-for-2027-water-prediction-innovators-summer-institute-theme-leaders).

**Graduate Student Fellows** work in teams with peers from across the country on water prediction research. The fellowship is open to current and incoming M.S. and Ph.D. students at U.S. universities. Applications close January 12, 2027. [Student Fellow application](https://www.cuahsi.org/summer-institute/student-fellow-applications).

To learn more first, join the [Summer Institute Impact and Overview Webinar](/community/events/wpi-summer-institute-webinar-2026) with the NOAA-NWS Office of Water Prediction on December 3. Summer Institute alumni, and students and Theme Leaders interested in taking part in 2027, are also invited to a Summer Institute gathering at AGU on December 9, 8:00 to 9:00 pm PT at Underdogs Cantina, after the [CUAHSI Community Gathering](/community/events/cuahsi-agu-gathering-2026). [RSVP for the Summer Institute gathering](https://cuahsi.jotform.com/262956813966878).

Questions: contact Julia Masterman at jmasterman@cuahsi.org. Covered in the [November 2026 e-newsletter](/community/newsletter/2026-november).
