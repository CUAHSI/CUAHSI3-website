The next reflection in the 25th anniversary series comes from **Hoori Ajami**, CUAHSI Board of Directors Chair-Elect for 2027 and Professor of Groundwater Hydrology at the University of California, Riverside.

Ajami first heard about CUAHSI at her first AGU Fall Meeting in 2007, when her co-mentor urged her to attend the Hydrology Section and CUAHSI reception. A focused networking event that put weight on graduate students made an impression, and she attended every year afterward. She became formally involved in 2021 through CUAHSI's new Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion committee, where she helped revisit the criteria for some student grants to reach underrepresented groups, and joined the Board in January 2023. Her board work has included the strategic plan, the Education and Outreach Committee, the bylaws revision and the Executive Committee.

Looking ahead, she sees CUAHSI's role moving from infrastructure for data repositories and sharing toward a broader ecosystem for open science, reproducibility and advanced computational infrastructure for teaching and research. As she steps into the chair role, she hopes to grow membership and deepen involvement across the water science community. She also names the Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute and the Snow Measurement Field School as programs worth continued support.
