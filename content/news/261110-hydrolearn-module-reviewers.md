CUAHSI is hiring two part-time reviewers to help review and document modules on [HydroLearn](https://hydrolearn.org/), an open-source platform for interactive hydrology and water resources learning content. Reviewers will complete modules, catalog their topics and skills, and help guide the development of new content.

- **Reports to:** Courtney Di Vittorio, Education Scholar
- **Position type:** Part-time (5 to 35 hours per week) through March 30, 2027, with potential for extension

## Position summary

HydroLearn Module Reviewers support CUAHSI's education and outreach portfolio by reviewing and documenting modules on HydroLearn, an open-source platform for creating, sharing and using interactive, modular learning content in hydrology and water resources. HydroLearn currently hosts 86 modules; early modules were developed with NSF support, and recent modules were supported by NOAA CIROH (Cooperative Institute for Research to Operations in Hydrology). Working closely with the project lead, Dr. Courtney Di Vittorio, reviewers complete modules, document their reviews, catalog module topics, skills and learning activities, and occasionally update content. This work will inform module discovery tools, identify needs and gaps, and guide the development of new content. The position is well suited to undergraduate and graduate students who want to learn while helping advance water science education.

## Key responsibilities

**Module review and documentation**

- Complete and review modules on the HydroLearn platform.
- Document reviews using project-specific templates.
- Catalog the topics, skills and learning activities contained in each module.
- Provide documentation that supports the development of module discovery tools and the identification of needs and gaps.

**Content maintenance**

- Occasionally update module content as directed by the project lead.
- Contribute findings that inform planning for new content.

**Communication and project coordination**

- Meet virtually with the project lead to check in and discuss next steps.
- Follow through on agreed-upon tasks and timelines.

## Required qualifications

- Completing or have completed an undergraduate or graduate degree in a field related to hydrology (civil engineering, environmental science, geology, etc.).

## Required skills

- Excellent organizational abilities and attention to detail.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
- Ability to effectively manage tasks and deadlines.
- Comfort using relevant software to complete learning activities, such as hydrologic and hydraulic modeling software (HEC-RAS, SWMM) and GIS (ArcGIS, QGIS).
- Comfort using open science programming and modeling tools, such as Jupyter notebooks, Google Colab and CUAHSI cloud computing resources.

## Working conditions

- Remote work environment; reliable internet access, a personal computer and comfort with virtual collaboration tools (video meetings, shared documents and cloud-based platforms) are essential.
- Weekly hours vary between 5 and 35 depending on project needs and reviewer availability, and are agreed with the project lead in advance.
- Work is largely self-directed, with flexibility in when tasks are completed, provided agreed deadlines and deliverables are met.
- Regular virtual check-ins with the project lead to review progress, discuss questions and plan next steps.
- Work involves extended computer-based tasks, including completing interactive learning activities, using modeling and GIS software, and writing structured documentation.
- Responsibilities may shift as the project evolves, and reviewers may be asked to take on additional modules or tasks within the scope of the position.
- The appointment runs through March 30, 2027, with potential for extension based on project needs and funding.
