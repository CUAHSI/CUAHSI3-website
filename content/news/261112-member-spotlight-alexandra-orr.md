Congratulations to Alexandra Orr, who earned her PhD at Penn State in 2025, advised by Beth Boyer, and is now Assistant Laboratory Professor of Biology at Bucknell University. Her doctoral work on an urbanizing karst watershed was partly supported by the NSF Critical Zone Collaborative Network Hub award (EAR-2012893), which CUAHSI holds, and all three of her dissertation papers were published in 2026, each acknowledging the award:

- [Streamflow Dynamics Across an Urbanizing Karst Watershed in the Ridge and Valley Province](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025WR041126), *Water Resources Research*
- [Road Salt Impacts on Streams and Springs in a Karstic Watershed](https://doi.org/10.1088/2515-7620/ae6035), *Environmental Research Communications*
- [Thermal Regime Dynamics in a Karst Watershed Influenced by Groundwater and Urbanization](https://doi.org/10.1002/hyp.70689), *Hydrological Processes*

The three papers are also listed among the [Critical Zone papers](/critical-zone-science/papers). Covered in the [November 2026 e-newsletter](/community/newsletter/2026-november).
