CUAHSI member [American Institute of Hydrology (AIH)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aihydrology/) is offering the AIH Fundamental Exam to qualified students and early-career professionals with no upfront exam or application fees from September to December 2026. It is an opportunity to demonstrate your knowledge, begin building your professional credentials, and take an important step toward becoming an AIH Hydrologist-in-Training (HIT).

There is no upfront cost to take the exam. If you pass the Part I (Fundamentals) exam and choose to accept your AIH HIT certification, the exam fee is discounted 50%. If you do not pass, you pay no exam fee. [Learn more about the exam on the AIH website](https://aihydrology.org/youre-ready-take-the-exam/).
