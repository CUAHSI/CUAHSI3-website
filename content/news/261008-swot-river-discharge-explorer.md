Punwath Prum, a Graduate Student Researcher at the University of Pittsburgh, has launched the [SWOT River Discharge Explorer](https://punwath.github.io/swot-river-discharge-explorer/), a web application for exploring, visualizing and downloading river discharge time series for North American rivers.

The app is built on satellite-derived data from the Surface Water and Ocean Topography (SWOT) mission, the Hydrocron service from NASA's Physical Oceanography Distributed Active Archive Center (PO.DAAC), and the SWOT River Database (SWORD) river network. It supports researchers and water resource professionals in identifying hydrologic trends and understanding how rivers respond to environmental change.

Prum's earlier work with CUAHSI is described in [his HydroInformatics Innovation Fellowship story](/about/impact/punwath-prum-hif-2026). Covered in the [October 2026 e-newsletter](/community/newsletter/2026-october).
