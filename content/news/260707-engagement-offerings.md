CUAHSI staff are offering to bring a talk or training to campuses and classrooms, in person or virtually. The sessions are tailored to the audience, introduce water researchers and students to CUAHSI programs and tools, support data-ready research practices, and help CUAHSI connect with new communities. Requests are welcome from member institutions, prospective members and the broader water science community.

To schedule a visit, write to connect@cuahsi.org or contact a CUAHSI staff member directly.

## The menu of sessions

- **Introduction to CUAHSI talk** (1 hour, seminar or webinar): an overview of CUAHSI programs, data tools and services, tailored to your department. It suits department seminars, new graduate student orientation and faculty meetings.
- **Hands-on water data tools workshop** (1 to 2 hours): finding data, uploading and sharing datasets in HydroShare, and running analyses in JupyterHub. Suited to graduate courses and research groups starting a project.
- **Student-focused career panel and discussion** (1 to 2 hours): CUAHSI staff talk about career paths in water science, as a panel with student questions or an informal conversation about day-to-day work as a research or data scientist, software developer, or education and community support specialist.
- **FAIR data management and reproducible workflows workshop** (2 to 3 hours): data management planning, the FAIR principles and reproducible workflow design with HydroShare and Jupyter, for researchers preparing to publish data or meet NSF public access requirements.
- **One-on-one or small group consultation** (flexible): direct support from a CUAHSI data scientist, researcher or technologist, for example when preparing a data management plan or bringing CUAHSI services into a curriculum.
