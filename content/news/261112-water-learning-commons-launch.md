The [CUAHSI Water Learning Commons](https://water-learning-commons.cuahsi.org/) is a free, open collection of water science teaching materials made by and for the community: full courses, code notebooks and workshop activities that instructors can use or adapt. It is built so instructors can reuse each other's materials, and so authors get credit and can see how their work is used.

The collection so far includes JP Gannon's Hydroinformatics course, Mark Bakker and Vincent Post's Analytical Groundwater Modeling course, Keira Johnson's introductory hydrology workshop built around the interactive app HydroViz, and several notebooks curated by CUAHSI with reusable workflows for open water data. Everything is openly licensed, and resources hosted on the platform are archived and citable through HydroShare. Field protocols, reproducible workflow examples and real-world impact stories are planned for the coming months.

**Share your teaching materials.** If you have built courseware, field protocols, notebooks or other teaching materials, CUAHSI would like to host them and help you reach more people. You keep ownership and can keep working where you already do. Authors also receive an Authorship Impact Report showing how the community uses their materials, useful for planning revisions and for CVs or annual reviews. To add materials or learn more, email help@cuahsi.org with a link and a short description.
