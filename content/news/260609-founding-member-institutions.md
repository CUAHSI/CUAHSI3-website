As part of the 25th anniversary, CUAHSI looked back at the 33 universities that became its founding members when the consortium was incorporated in 2001. They came together on the shared belief that advancing hydrologic science needed infrastructure no single institution could support alone.

Representatives of all 33 institutions were elected to the consortium's first Board of Directors, which shaped the governance and direction of the new organization.

In the 25 years since, CUAHSI's consortium membership has more than tripled. Changes to the bylaws have widened who can join, opening participation to a broader range of organizations, and member representatives continue to guide CUAHSI's strategic direction.
