During 2026 CUAHSI took over hosting the archive of the **Women Advancing River Research** (WARR) webinar series. WARR began in February 2021 as a monthly series with women guest speakers from around the world, originally co-hosted and organized by Professor Li Li at Penn State University and Professor Ellen Wohl at Colorado State University. The archive is now on CUAHSI's [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@CUAHSI) and its [WARR series page](https://www.cuahsi.org/cyberseminars/series/women-advancing-river-research-lecture-series), and CUAHSI will facilitate the series fully beginning in 2027.

The fall 2026 sessions are on the events page: [October 15](/community/events/women-advancing-river-research-hydroclimate-2026) and [November 19](/community/events/women-advancing-river-research-bedrock-2026).
