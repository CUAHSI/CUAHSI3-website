CUAHSI hosted the *Stakeholder-Informed Spatial Modeling for Hydrologic Sciences* workshop August 18–19, 2025 in Nahant, Massachusetts, co-organized with instructors from Indiana University–Bloomington and Northeastern University.

The workshop addressed two skill gaps for early-career researchers: integrating spatial and hydrologic sciences for modeling applications, and engaging model users in designing model outcomes and communication strategies that are meaningful to those users, not just scientifically valid. The combination of technical and science communication training reflects CUAHSI's view that water models only create impact when their outputs can be understood and acted on by the communities they serve.

Demand significantly exceeded capacity: 68 people applied for 15 participant slots, with the final cohort representing 13 unique institutions. Participant feedback indicated strong satisfaction and intent to apply the skills directly to their research.

At 4.5 applicants per available spot, the workshop represents one of the clearest signals in CUAHSI's recent programming of unmet demand for training at the science-society interface in hydrology.

The workshop is described on its [CUAHSI workshop page](https://www.cuahsi.org/workshops/stakeholder-informed-spatial-modeling-for-hydrologic-sciences). Covered in the [CUAHSI 2025 Annual Report](/documents/annual-reports/annual-report-2025.pdf).
