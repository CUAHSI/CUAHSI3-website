In January 2026 CUAHSI staff (CEO Jordan Read, Lead of Software Engineering Martin Seul, Lead of Research Tony Castronova and Senior Software Engineer Scott Black) met Utah State University partners Dave Tarboton, Jeff Horsburgh, Homa Salehabadi, Pabitra Dash, Ayman Nassar and Maurier Ramirez in Logan, Utah. The meeting was a soft launch of a five-year roadmapping effort for the HydroShare ecosystem, aimed at a shared understanding of HydroShare's risks and opportunities and early priorities rather than final decisions. Monthly strategic coordination calls followed through August 2026, and CUAHSI staff will continue to build the full roadmap. It is part of the sustainability work under [NSF award 2535162](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=2535162), "Sustained Resources: Advancing Water Science Through Integrated Water Data Management and Community Support" (January 2026 to December 2028).
