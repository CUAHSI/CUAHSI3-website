CUAHSI piloted its first Data Publishing Cohort Program in fall 2025, providing structured peer support for researchers preparing their data for publication. The pilot addressed a consistent gap identified through CUAHSI's help desk: while researchers want to publish their data, many feel overwhelmed by the requirements for FAIR metadata, file organization, and documentation.

Over three weeks, 10 selected participants (from 16 applicants) attended five webinar sessions covering metadata fundamentals, file organization strategies, quality control documentation, and how to leverage cloud computing resources for data preparation. Participants also had the option to schedule a 30-minute one-on-one consulting session with a CUAHSI staff member for specialized assistance with their specific dataset.

A small participation stipend was offered to recognize participants' time and reduce barriers to engagement. The program concluded with a listening session in December 2025 to collect feedback on the experience and identify improvements for future cohorts.

The cohort model — structured curriculum plus individualized support — represents a more scalable approach to data publishing assistance than the help desk alone, and is designed to expand in subsequent years under the new cooperative agreement (EAR-2535162).

The program continues: see the [Data Publishing Program](/community/events/data-publishing-program-fall-2026). Covered in the [CUAHSI 2025 Annual Report](/documents/annual-reports/annual-report-2025.pdf).
