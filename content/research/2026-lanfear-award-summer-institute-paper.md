The American Water Resources Association gave its 2026 [Kenneth J. Lanfear Award for Outstanding Technology Paper](https://www.awra.org/AWRA/Members/Scholarship_and_Awards/Award_Descriptions/Kenneth_Lanfear_Award.aspx) to "[Machine Learning for a Heterogeneous Water Modeling Framework](https://doi.org/10.1111/1752-1688.70000)," published in the *Journal of the American Water Resources Association* in 2025. The award is given each year for an outstanding technology paper in the journal.

The paper grew out of research that spanned three [Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institutes](/learn-train/programs/summer-institute), from 2021 to 2023, where graduate student teams work on operational water prediction. Several of its authors are Summer Institute alumni who have since finished their degrees and now work as assistant professors, at NASA and NOAA, as postdocs and at private engineering firms.

The authors are Jonathan M. Frame, Ryoko Araki, Soelem Aafnan Bhuiyan, Tadd Bindas, Jeremy Rapp, Lauren Bolotin, Emily Deardorff, Qiyue Liu, Francisco Haces-Garcia, Mochi Liao, Nels Frazier and Fred L. Ogden. The paper's record lists funding from the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration.

Applications for the 2027 Summer Institute are [open now](/community/news/wpi-2027-applications-open). Covered in the [November 2026 e-newsletter](/community/newsletter/2026-november).
