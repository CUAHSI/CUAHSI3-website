CUAHSI established a functional prototype of the [Water Content Portal](https://water-content-portal.cuahsi.io/), an open-access community platform for sharing and maintaining curated educational and scientific resources in hydrology and water science.

The portal uses open-source technologies — JupyterBook, MyST, and DecapCMS — to enable community contribution and content versioning. Community members retain ownership and control of their content in their own GitHub repositories, while CUAHSI curates which content is made available through the portal to ensure quality. A continuous deployment pipeline via Netlify allows contributors to preview and test their content before publication.

A significant milestone was the integration of J.P. Gannon's (Virginia Tech) Hydroinformatics textbook — a 17-chapter resource covering hydrologic data analysis, Python programming, and reproducible workflows. Gannon used the portal-hosted version as a teaching resource for one of his spring 2026 courses. The textbook is publicly available at: https://water-content-portal.cuahsi.io/hydroinformatics/

Additional content on the portal includes an initial version of a Data Collection Protocols textbook developed with CUAHSI's Instrumentation and Data Acquisition Advisory Committee.
