CUAHSI collaborated with Admin Husic of Virginia Tech to archive and publish a comprehensive high-frequency sensor database of water quality in U.S. rivers, called STREAM. The outcomes are public on HydroShare as "STREAM: a Comprehensive High-Frequency Sensor Database of Water Quality in U.S. Rivers" and "STREAM: Analysis-Ready Parquet Data for High-Frequency Water Quality in U.S. Rivers". The work falls under data publishing support in [NSF award 2535162](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=2535162), "Sustained Resources: Advancing Water Science Through Integrated Water Data Management and Community Support" (January 2026 to December 2028).
