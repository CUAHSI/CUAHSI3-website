In April 2026, CUAHSI Environmental Data Science Fellow Kimmy Wong attended the U.S. Indigenous Data Sovereignty & Governance Summit in Tucson, Arizona. The summit brought together Indigenous data practitioners, researchers, and policymakers to advance frameworks for community control over data about Indigenous peoples and lands.

Wong attended to better understand Indigenous community data needs and to learn about the CARE Maturity Model: a framework for evaluating how well data governance systems align with the principles of Collective Benefit, Authority to Control, Responsibility, and Ethics. The CARE principles complement FAIR data principles and are increasingly relevant to CUAHSI's work on open data infrastructure for water science communities, particularly in watersheds and landscapes where Indigenous water rights and knowledge are central.

The summit is the [U.S. Indigenous Data Sovereignty & Governance Summit 2026](https://s2.goeshow.com/usidsn/summit/2026/index.cfm). Covered in the [May 2026 e-newsletter](/community/newsletter/2026-may).
