CUAHSI and the University of Alabama announced the participants for the 2026 Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute, the program's latest cohort bringing graduate students together with academic researchers and operational water prediction professionals over seven weeks.

The 2026 cohort includes 24+ student fellows from universities across the United States, working on projects that advance water prediction and flood forecasting capabilities. The program is supported by the Cooperative Institute for Research to Operations in Hydrology (CIROH) with funding from NOAA under award NA22NWS4320003.

The Summer Institute is one of CUAHSI's most direct pipelines from graduate training to operational impact — alumni have contributed to improvements in National Water Model flood inundation mapping and to the cyberinfrastructure that disseminates NWM outputs to researchers and practitioners.

## Capstone and outcomes

![A group of fellows, theme leaders and staff pose in a lecture hall in front of a projected slide reading Water Prediction Innovators Summer Institute, Capstone Event, July 22, 2026.](/newsletter-images/2026-august/wpi-si-capstone-2026.jpg)

The 2026 Summer Institute ran from June 8 to July 22, 2026. Twenty-four graduate student fellows from 21 unique U.S. institutions formed research teams under the guidance of seven Theme Leaders, on topics from precipitation forecasting to flood risk communication. The program is a collaboration between CUAHSI, The University of Alabama, the Alabama Water Institute and NOAA's Office of Water Prediction, and this was its 11th year. It draws more than 100 applicants a year, and its fellows and alumni represent more than 100 universities.

The Capstone took place on July 22, 2026 at The University of Alabama in Tuscaloosa. Fellows presented their research to an audience that included representatives from the NOAA Office of Water Prediction, CIROH and The University of Alabama, as well as the fellows' advisors and peers. CEO Jordan Read and Community Relations and Training Coordinator Julia Masterman attended. The research outcomes are summarized in the [2026 Summer Institute final report](https://www.hydroshare.org/resource/7b2b22d88a914bb79b3f48e5ea3ecaaf/) on HydroShare.
