In 2026 CUAHSI set out to reach universities and colleges that have little or no prior connection with it. Staff met in person on April 22 and 23 in Madison, Wisconsin for an internal Engagement Summit, which produced about 15 targeted efforts and several resources:

- an outreach template that sets out the ways to engage with CUAHSI (see [CUAHSI Engagement Offerings](/community/news/engagement-offerings-2026));
- an approach of contacting university librarians to set up informational sessions, which led to an Introduction to HydroShare presentation at the University of Pennsylvania given by Environmental Data Science Fellow Kimmy Wong;
- a list of 25 institutions with water graduate programs and little or no engagement with CUAHSI, drawn from a hydrology-focused subset of the federal IPEDS data; and
- lists of US authors publishing in water science journals, and US principal investigators with past Hydrologic Sciences awards, who have no recent engagement with CUAHSI.

Visits made this year:

- **Hamilton College** (a primarily undergraduate institution), February 10: Research Analyst Abner Bogan led an interactive session on finding data in HydroShare for an Environmental Science course and gave an open science talk to the Environmental Studies Seminar.
- **University of Florida**, March 25 and 26: Wong led a HydroShare workshop at the UF Water Institute student showcase.
- **University of Iowa, Iowa Institute of Hydraulic Research**, April 15: CEO Jordan Read gave a CUAHSI seminar and met faculty and research staff.
- **Iowa State University**, April 16: a visit organized by the university's CUAHSI member representatives, with roundtables with faculty from five departments, a student lunch and a seminar.
- **University of Nebraska-Lincoln**, April 17: meetings with the university's member representatives and a lunch with faculty and administrators.

To request a visit, see the [campus visits page](/community/campus-visits).
