Between January and August 2026 CUAHSI hosted two cyberseminar series, six webinars in all. Both were free, open to the public and archived on [CUAHSI's recordings page](/learn-train/cyberseminars).

**Integrating Hydrology and Geophysics** (January 28 to March 11, 2026) was a joint series with the EarthScope-operated NSF National Geophysical Facility, to bring geophysicists and hydrologists together and broaden critical zone science communities.

- January 28: Current research in hydrogeodesy and hydroseismology (310 live views, 442 archived)
- February 11: Challenges and opportunities in data sharing and critical zone science (256 live views, 195 archived)
- February 25: Innovative technical tools and methods (190 live views, 400 archived)
- March 11: Building skills and finding collaborators for hydrogeophysics (165 live views, 246 archived)

**Introduction to NEON for Hydrology** (March 4 to April 1, 2026) gave an overview of the National Ecological Observatory Network's hydrologic data products and tools.

- March 4: An overview of hydrologic data products and tools (105 live views, 187 archived)
- April 1: Data skills demo: exploring the water cycle at co-located terrestrial-aquatic sites (85 live views, 155 archived)

Across the six webinars that is 1,111 live views and 1,625 archived views (counts as of the report in September 2026, and archived views keep growing). The NEON series also produced two lasting outcomes: a collection of NEON hydrologic data products published to HydroShare (see [the NEON collection](/about/impact/neon-hydroshare-collection-2026)), and NEON-authored code-lab notebooks contributed to CUAHSI's open learning platform.

The series are supported by [NSF award 2535162](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=2535162), "Sustained Resources: Advancing Water Science Through Integrated Water Data Management and Community Support" (January 2026 to December 2028). More: [EarthScope](https://www.earthscope.org/) and [NEON](https://www.neonscience.org/).
