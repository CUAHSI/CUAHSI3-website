The HIVIS Hydro-Haze Dataset (Henein, Ayyad, and Temimi) presents ground-based USGS streamgage camera images from nine Alaska stations, documenting image quality degradation caused by haze, fog, precipitation, and other harsh environmental conditions common to Alaska field sites.

The resource applies diffusion-leveraged generative adversarial network (GAN) dehazing driven by image quality classification: a machine learning approach that identifies and corrects for atmospheric interference in streamgage imagery. The dataset includes transparent documentation of quality categorization and processing methods, making it both a scientific contribution and a model for how environmental monitoring image data can be published on HydroShare in a way that supports reuse and reproducibility.

CUAHSI featured it as the February 2026 HydroShare Data Spotlight for how well it documents its data and metadata.

The dataset is on HydroShare: [HIVIS Hydro-Haze Dataset: Diffusion-Leveraged GAN Dehazing Driven by Classification](https://www.hydroshare.org/resource/2aca7bf88a944b86af4cd4c4b01bed71/).
