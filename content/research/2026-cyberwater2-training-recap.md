In the fall of 2026, CUAHSI and the CyberWater2 project team at the University of Pittsburgh and Indiana University Indianapolis ran a [virtual training](/community/events/cyberwater2-training-2026) on [CyberWater](https://cyber-water.luddy.indianapolis.iu.edu/), a framework for linking models, data and computing tools into reproducible hydrologic modeling workflows. Forty-seven participants from at least 24 institutions in five countries took part.

Through live demonstrations, discussions and hands-on exercises, participants built and ran data-intensive modeling workflows. They could also draft and share a short proposal for using CyberWater in their own research, or for extending it with other models and tools.

CyberWater2 is a collaborative project of the University of Pittsburgh, Indiana University and Ball State University; CUAHSI takes part through a subaward from the University of Pittsburgh, named below.

The lecture recordings are free in the [CUAHSI cyberseminar archive](/learn-train/cyberseminars) and in the CyberWater playlist on [CUAHSI's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLCBF49uU4qHM). Covered in the [November 2026 e-newsletter](/community/newsletter/2026-november).
