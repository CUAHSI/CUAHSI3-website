Colorado State University is hiring a Water Rights Specialist to support a National Park Service agreement focused on protecting park water resources. The role involves researching and analyzing water rights, preparing inventories, identifying legal vulnerabilities, and recommending management actions. Candidates must have a JD with 1 year of experience or a relevant Master's degree with 2 years of experience, along with in-depth knowledge of US water law and GIS proficiency.
