The Washington State Department of Ecology's Water Quality Program is hiring a project specialist (funded through December 31, 2029) to manage grants and loans for Puget Sound nutrient reduction efforts at wastewater treatment plants. The role involves developing funding agreements, monitoring project compliance, conducting site visits, and supporting environmental justice initiatives. Candidates need at least 5 years of relevant experience plus a bachelor's degree (or equivalent combination) in an environmental or natural science field.
