NRCS is hiring student interns under the Pathways Student Internship Program for paid, career-oriented work in biological sciences, soil conservation, and natural resource management. Applicants must be enrolled at least half-time in an accredited institution pursuing a degree or certificate in biological sciences, agriculture, natural resource management, chemistry, or a related field. Positions range from GS-02 to GS-04 based on education level, with potential for non-competitive conversion to a permanent GS-0401 position upon meeting program requirements.
