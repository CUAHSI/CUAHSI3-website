The Bureau of Reclamation is hiring a Hydrologist at the GS-07 through GS-11 level to support water resource management in the western United States. The role involves collecting, analyzing, and modeling hydrologic data, conducting watershed studies, and coordinating with state, local, and tribal entities on water quality and quantity issues. Applicants must have a qualifying degree in a physical or natural science with specific coursework in calculus and physics, plus relevant specialized experience or graduate education.
