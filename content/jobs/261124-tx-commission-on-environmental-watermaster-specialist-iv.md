TCEQ is hiring a full-time Watermaster Specialist IV (Technical Deputy) for the Brazos Watermaster Program within its Water Availability Division. The role involves monitoring surface water use, reviewing water accounting data, conducting field inspections, and ensuring compliance with water rights rules in the Brazos River Basin. Candidates need a bachelor's degree in a natural/physical science, engineering, or related field plus five years of environmental experience (or equivalent education substitutions).
