The UC Irvine Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering is hiring a postdoctoral scholar for the UCI Flood Lab to study river and coastal morphodynamics in Southern California. The researcher will develop and apply Delft3D models of the Santa Ana River and Orange County coast to evaluate sediment nourishment strategies for restoring engineered sediment systems. A Ph.D. in Civil Engineering, Coastal Engineering, Geomorphology, or a related field is required.
