Texas State University is recruiting a fully funded PhD student for Fall 2027 to study quantitative hydrology, including watershed modeling, flow-regime analysis, and climate and land-use change impacts on river systems. The position is within a research group focused on hydrologic variability and freshwater ecosystems. Preferred candidates have backgrounds in hydrology, water resources, or related fields with experience in hydrologic modeling, GIS, R, or Python.
