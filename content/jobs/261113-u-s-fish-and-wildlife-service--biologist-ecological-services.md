The U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service is hiring a Biologist for its Anchorage Fish & Wildlife Conservation Office to conduct ecological investigations, review permits, develop wildlife management plans, and prepare environmental assessments related to land and water resource development projects. The position is filled at the GS-9 or GS-11 level with a full performance level of GS-11. Applicants must hold a degree in biological sciences or a related field and have specialized experience in fish and wildlife biology, ecology, or conservation biology.
