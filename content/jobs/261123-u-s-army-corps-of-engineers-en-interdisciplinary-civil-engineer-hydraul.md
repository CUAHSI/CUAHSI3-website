The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers New Orleans District is hiring a technical subject matter expert in hydraulics, hydrology, or physical science at the GS-7 through GS-12 level. The role involves analyzing hydrologic and meteorological conditions, performing post-flood evaluations, establishing water control criteria, and modeling multi-project operational networks. Candidates must meet OPM basic education requirements for the Civil Engineering (0810), Physical Science (1301), or Hydrology (1315) series, plus relevant specialized experience appropriate to the grade applied for.
