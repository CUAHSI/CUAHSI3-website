The California State Water Resources Control Board is hiring an Environmental Scientist in its Bay-Delta Section to perform aquatic and fisheries analyses, research, and modeling related to the San Francisco Bay/Sacramento-San Joaquin River Delta. The role supports regulatory and planning activities for the Division of Water Rights, including review of hydrologic modeling and assessment of water management impacts on flows, fisheries, and water quality. Candidates must meet California state civil service eligibility requirements for the Environmental Scientist classification.
