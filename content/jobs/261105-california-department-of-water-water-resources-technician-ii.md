The California Department of Water Resources is hiring a Water Resources Technician II for its Southern Region Office Hydrological Data Section in Glendale. The role involves hydrologic data collection, field instrumentation installation and maintenance, streamflow measurement, groundwater monitoring, and support for State Water Project and Dam Safety Services activities. Applicants must meet the Water Resources Technician II classification requirements and possess a valid California Driver License.
