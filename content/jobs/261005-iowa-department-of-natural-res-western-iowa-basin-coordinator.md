The Iowa Department of Natural Resources is hiring an Environmental Specialist Senior to coordinate watershed planning and water quality improvement projects across western Iowa river basins. The role involves leading partnerships with local governments, conservation districts, and landowners, managing grants and contracts, and providing technical expertise in watershed assessments and best management practices. Candidates should have experience in environmental planning, watershed management, or a related field, with strong project management and communication skills.
