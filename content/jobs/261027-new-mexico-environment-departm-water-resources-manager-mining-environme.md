The New Mexico Environment Department is hiring a Program Manager for the Mining Environmental Compliance Section of the Ground Water Quality Bureau. The role involves leading technical staff, overseeing mine-site discharge permitting and groundwater pollution abatement under the New Mexico Water Quality Act, and ensuring regulatory compliance. A bachelor's degree in a water resources-related field plus eight years of relevant experience (six in a supervisory role) is required.
