The New Mexico Environment Department is hiring a Program Manager for the Pollution Prevention Section of the Ground Water Quality Bureau to oversee discharge permitting, corrective actions, and supervision of approximately twenty staff. The role involves reviewing permits under the New Mexico Water Quality Act, preparing technical testimony, and improving program effectiveness for domestic and industrial sites. A bachelor's degree in a water resources-related field and eight years of relevant experience, including six years in a supervisory role, is required.
