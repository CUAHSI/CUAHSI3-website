The USGS California Water Science Center is hiring Supervisory Hydrologic Technicians at the GS-12 level in Sacramento and West Sacramento, CA. The role involves supervising staff, overseeing hydrologic data collection and instrumentation, managing field projects, and ensuring scientific integrity. Applicants must have one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-11, including independently operating hydrologic instrumentation and providing technical guidance to staff.
