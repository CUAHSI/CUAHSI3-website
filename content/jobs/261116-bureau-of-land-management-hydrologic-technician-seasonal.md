The Bureau of Land Management is hiring seasonal Hydrologic Technicians at the GS-5 through GS-7 grade levels with an anticipated start of January–March 2027. The role involves installing and maintaining hydrologic equipment, collecting surface water and groundwater data, and managing water resources data using GIS and databases. Candidates must have relevant specialized experience or education in hydrology, engineering, physical science, or a related field.
