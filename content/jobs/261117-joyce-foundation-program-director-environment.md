The Joyce Foundation is hiring a Program Director to lead its Environment Program, overseeing a team of two Program Officers and one Program Assistant in executing a 2027–2031 strategic plan focused on climate, drinking water, and Great Lakes policy. The role involves grantmaking, strategy development, people management, and stakeholder engagement across the Great Lakes region. Candidates need at least 10 years of relevant experience in climate, water, or Great Lakes policymaking plus five years in grantmaking or resource allocation.
