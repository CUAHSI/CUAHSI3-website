The University of Toronto's research group led by Professor Richard Taylor is hiring a Post-Doctoral Fellow to investigate groundwater's role in climate change adaptation in Ontario and across Africa and Asia. The position involves analyzing groundwater-level and quality responses to changing water demand and precipitation extremes using observational databases, Earth Observation data, and hydrological models. Key qualifications include statistical methods, Python or R coding, GIS tools, and Google Earth Engine, with machine learning and hydrogeological experience desirable.
