UMass Dartmouth's Department of Estuarine and Ocean Sciences is hiring a full-time Research Technician to support coastal, estuarine, freshwater, and watershed research and monitoring. The role involves field sampling, laboratory analysis, equipment operation, data management, and assisting with grant preparation. A bachelor's degree in marine science, environmental science, or a related field plus hands-on field or laboratory research experience is required.
