Utah State University's Department of Agricultural and Resource Economics is hiring a tenure-track Assistant Professor specializing in natural resource economics with a focus on water economics and policy relevant to Utah and the Intermountain West. The position is 50% teaching and 45% research, requiring instruction in environmental and natural resource economics and development of a nationally recognized research program. Applicants must hold a Ph.D. in economics or a closely related field and demonstrate potential for productive research and effective teaching.
