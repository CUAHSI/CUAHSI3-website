George Mason University's Mason Flood Hazards Research Lab is hiring a Research Scientist to conduct flood and coastal hazards research using numerical modeling, geospatial analysis, and field monitoring across projects in Virginia, Maryland, and Arctic Alaska. The role involves community-engaged research, sensor network maintenance, student mentoring, and contribution to publications and proposals. A Ph.D. in a relevant engineering or earth science field and demonstrated experience in numerical modeling or geospatial analysis of flood hazards are required.
