The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers is hiring a GS-13 senior specialist to lead sediment transport, fluvial geomorphology, and river mechanics studies for the New Orleans District. The role involves numerical and physical modeling, team leadership, and project coordination across complex hydraulic and hydrologic systems. Applicants must hold a Professional Engineer or Professional Hydrologist license and have at least one year of specialized experience at the GS-12 level or equivalent.
