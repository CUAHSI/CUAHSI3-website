The University of Oregon Department of Earth Sciences is hiring an endowed Knight Chair at the Associate or Full Professor level in atmospheric and hydrologic processes for environmental resilience. The role requires a PhD in Earth Sciences or a related field, a tenurable record of scholarly excellence, and demonstrated leadership in interdisciplinary research connecting atmospheric, hydrologic, and Earth-system processes to societal challenges. The successful candidate will maintain an externally funded research program, mentor students, and teach at both undergraduate and graduate levels.
