Princeton University is seeking Ph.D. students to develop Tiger-HLM, a hydrologic land model, through work in hydrologic modeling, Earth system processes, and scientific machine learning. Candidates will contribute to advancing computational and data-driven approaches in hydrology. Strong backgrounds in relevant quantitative or computational fields are expected.
