The Institute for Water and Health at Georgia Southern University is hiring a postdoctoral researcher to lead groundwater modeling and hydrogeology projects focused on coastal aquifers and saltwater intrusion in Southeast Georgia. The role involves numerical groundwater flow modeling (e.g., MODFLOW), field monitoring, solute transport modeling, and data analysis in R or Python. A PhD in hydrogeology, hydrology, civil/environmental engineering, geology, or a closely related field is required, along with demonstrated experience in groundwater modeling and field data collection.
