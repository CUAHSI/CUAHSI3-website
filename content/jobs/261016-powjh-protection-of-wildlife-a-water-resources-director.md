POWJH is hiring a Water Resources Director to lead water quality science, policy, and infrastructure work across Teton County. The role requires expertise in limnology, hydrology, wastewater engineering, and western water law, including the Clean Water Act and prior appropriation doctrine. The candidate must also have experience securing grants from public and private sources.
