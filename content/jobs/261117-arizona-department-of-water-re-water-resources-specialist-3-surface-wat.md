The Arizona Department of Water Resources is hiring an experienced Water Resources Specialist for its Surface Water Section to process, review, and approve or reject surface water right applications, permits, certificates, and claims. The role involves researching and analyzing water resource management data, coordinating complex interdepartmental projects, and ensuring compliance with state and federal water law. Applicants need at least seven years of professional water resource management experience, or a relevant degree with proportionally fewer years of experience.
