Ducks Unlimited is hiring an Ecosystem Services Scientist for its Southern Regional Office in Ridgeland, MS. The role involves quantifying and communicating ecosystem-service benefits of wetland and working lands conservation, supporting funding development, and providing technical capacity across water quality, hydrology, and agricultural conservation programs. A Master's degree in a relevant environmental or natural resources field and experience applying water quality or wetland conservation concepts to real-world projects are required.
