The University of Idaho's College of Agricultural and Life Sciences is hiring a Program Manager I to support planning and administration of programs at the Idaho Water Resources Research Institute. The role involves coordinating program activities, managing budgets, contributing to grant proposals, and working with faculty, agencies, and stakeholders on water resource issues. Candidates need at least two years of program coordination experience and a bachelor's degree, with preference for backgrounds in water resources, natural sciences, or related fields.
