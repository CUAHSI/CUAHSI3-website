The California Department of Water Resources is hiring a permanent Engineer, Water Resources for its Reservoir Coordinated Operations Unit. The role involves real-time storm monitoring, river and reservoir inflow forecasting, hydrologic and reservoir operations modeling, and coordination with state and federal partners. Candidates must meet the Engineer, Water Resources classification minimum qualifications and hold a valid California Driver's License.
