Rutgers CAIT is hiring a Postdoctoral Associate to work in the Rutgers Flood Resilience Lab on hydrodynamic/hydraulic flood modeling and AI/ML applications for flood early warning, risk assessment, and infrastructure resilience. The role involves developing numerical flood models, applying machine learning for predictive forecasting, and processing large geospatial datasets under federally funded research programs. Candidates must hold a Ph.D. in Civil/Environmental Engineering, Water Resources, Earth Science, Computer Science, or a related field, with demonstrated experience in flood modeling and AI/ML methods.
