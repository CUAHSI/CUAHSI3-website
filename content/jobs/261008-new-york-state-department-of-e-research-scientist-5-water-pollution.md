The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation is hiring a Research Scientist 5 to direct the Bureau of Water Assessment and Management. The role involves leading water quality monitoring, research, and standards development programs, including harmful algal blooms, nutrient criteria, and emerging contaminants. Candidates must qualify for the non-competitive jurisdictional class and will manage bureau staff, fiscal resources, and federal grant commitments.
