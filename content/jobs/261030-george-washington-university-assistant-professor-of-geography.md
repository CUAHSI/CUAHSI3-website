The Department of Geography and Environment at George Washington University is hiring a tenure-track Assistant Professor starting Fall 2027. The position seeks an environmental scientist, physical geographer, or earth-system scientist whose research addresses climate and environmental change and human-Earth system interactions. Candidates must hold a PhD in geography, environmental science, or a related field and demonstrate research or teaching experience using field, quantitative, or geospatial methods.
