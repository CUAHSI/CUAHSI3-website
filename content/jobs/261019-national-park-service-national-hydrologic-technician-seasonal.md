The National Park Service is hiring seasonal Hydrologic Technicians at the GS-6 and GS-7 levels with an anticipated start between March and July 2027. The role involves collecting field hydrologic data, calibrating and programming water quality monitoring equipment, and managing data using specialized software. Applicants must have specialized experience in water-focused data collection and hydrologic monitoring, or qualifying graduate education at the GS-7 level.
