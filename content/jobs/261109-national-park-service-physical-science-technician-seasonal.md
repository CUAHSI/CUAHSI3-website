The National Park Service is hiring seasonal Physical Science Technicians at the GS-05/06/07 levels to support field and laboratory work in physical science monitoring programs. Duties include collecting air, water, and soil samples, operating scientific equipment, managing data, and assisting with environmental research. Candidates must have relevant specialized experience or a degree in a physical science field meeting OPM qualification standards for the applicable grade level.
