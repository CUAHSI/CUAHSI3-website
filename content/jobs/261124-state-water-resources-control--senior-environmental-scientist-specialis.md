The California State Water Resources Control Board's Division of Water Quality is hiring a Senior Environmental Scientist (Specialist) to develop statewide water quality control plans, policies, and guidance, with an early focus on harmful algal blooms. The role involves leading complex multi-year projects, managing staff reports and outreach documents, and engaging with internal and external stakeholders. Candidates must be eligible for the California civil service classification and reside in California upon appointment.
