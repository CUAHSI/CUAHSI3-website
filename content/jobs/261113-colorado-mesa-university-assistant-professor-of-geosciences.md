Colorado Mesa University is hiring a tenure-track Assistant Professor of Geosciences specializing in Hydrology, Geomorphology, and Watershed Science, starting August 2027. The position involves teaching introductory and upper-division geosciences courses, running an undergraduate research program, and participating in a summer field camp. A Ph.D. in Geosciences or a closely related discipline by summer 2027 and demonstrated expertise in water resources science are required.
