Salt Lake City Public Utilities is hiring a full-time Watershed Special Projects Coordinator to develop, coordinate, and monitor environmental and multi-jurisdictional projects in compliance with federal, state, and local regulations. The role involves managing consultants, preparing environmental compliance policies, and supporting long-range planning. Candidates must have a bachelor's degree in a related field and eight years of professional experience in environmental matters.
