The Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection is hiring a Water Program Specialist for its Bureau of Safe Drinking Water, Operations and Monitoring Division. The role involves evaluating field operations, supporting regulation development, reviewing data, and coordinating projects to reduce lead exposure in public water systems. Applicants must have three years of professional environmental protection experience and a bachelor's degree in a relevant science or engineering field, or an equivalent combination.
