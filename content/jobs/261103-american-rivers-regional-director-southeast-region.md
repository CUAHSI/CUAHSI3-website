American Rivers is hiring a Senior Director-level Regional Director to lead its Southeast region team and fundraising efforts focused on river conservation, restoration, and advocacy. The role involves supervising experienced staff and managers, personally securing philanthropic support, and translating organizational strategy into a regional conservation plan. Candidates must have at least 10 years of relevant experience, demonstrated success in major gift fundraising, and proven people-management skills.
