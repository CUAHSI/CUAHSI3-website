The National Park Service is hiring a seasonal Physical Science Technician (GS-7) at Big South Fork National River and Recreation Area to support field and laboratory work for physical science monitoring and resource management programs. Duties include collecting air, water, soil, and climate data; operating and maintaining scientific equipment; and preparing technical reports. Applicants must have one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-6, one year of relevant graduate education, or an equivalent combination.
