UC Davis Department of Agricultural & Resource Economics is hiring an Assistant Project Scientist to conduct research in agricultural and natural resource economics, with a focus on property rights, water resource management, and indigenous water policy under the supervision of Associate Professor Eric Edwards. The role involves research, publishing, and grant acquisition, with no teaching responsibilities. A Ph.D. in Economics or a related field is required.
