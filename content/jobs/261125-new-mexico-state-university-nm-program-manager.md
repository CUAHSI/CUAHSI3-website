New Mexico State University's Water Resources Research Institute is hiring a Program Manager to oversee administration of the Agricultural Water Resilience Program and other grant-funded initiatives. The role covers the full program lifecycle including grant and contract administration, budget monitoring, financial compliance, and performance reporting. Candidates need an associate's degree plus 7 years of relevant experience (or bachelor's plus 5 years), with strong background in sponsored project administration and financial compliance.
