The Alliance for the Great Lakes is hiring a Senior Source Water Policy Manager to lead research, policy analysis, and advocacy on water quantity and source water protection across the Great Lakes Basin. A bachelor's degree and at least seven years of experience in water policy, natural resources management, or a related field are required, with a graduate degree or JD preferred.
