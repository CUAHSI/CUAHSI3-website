The Department of Earth System Science at UC Irvine is hiring a postdoctoral scholar for a two-year NSF-funded project investigating the global spatial organization of precipitation, soil moisture, and vegetation using satellite observations and models. The scholar will analyze remote sensing data, develop methods for characterizing hydrologic and vegetation patterns across scales, and lead peer-reviewed publications. Applicants must have a PhD in hydrology, remote sensing, Earth system science, or a related field, along with experience in scientific programming, geospatial data analysis, and high-performance computing.
