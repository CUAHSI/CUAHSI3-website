Howard University's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering seeks a tenure-line faculty member at the Associate Professor rank (or Assistant Professor with strong qualifications) to hold the Eugene and Dorothy Krysuik Endowed Professorship. The role involves establishing an externally funded research program, teaching, and mentoring in environmental engineering and related civil engineering disciplines. An earned doctorate in Environmental Engineering, Civil Engineering, or a closely related field is required.
