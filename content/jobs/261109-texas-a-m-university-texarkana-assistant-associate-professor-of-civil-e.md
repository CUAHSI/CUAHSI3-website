Texas A&M University Texarkana's College of Engineering and Computing is hiring a full-time, tenure-track faculty member at the Assistant or Associate Professor level in Civil Engineering, with an anticipated start date of Fall 2027. The role involves teaching undergraduate and graduate civil engineering courses, establishing a research program, and contributing to ABET accreditation. An earned Ph.D. in civil engineering with a concentration in geotechnical, geoenvironmental, or environmental and water resources engineering is required.
