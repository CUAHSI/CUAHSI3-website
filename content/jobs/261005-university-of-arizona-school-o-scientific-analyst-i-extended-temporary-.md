The University of Arizona's Rangeland Hydrology and Erosion Model (RHEM) team is hiring a part-time Scientific Analyst I to maintain and improve web-based natural resource tools. The role involves data preprocessing, spatial analysis, Python scripting, and GIS work in support of rangeland hydrology and soil erosion research. A bachelor's degree in a related natural resource discipline and proficiency in Python and ArcGIS are required.
