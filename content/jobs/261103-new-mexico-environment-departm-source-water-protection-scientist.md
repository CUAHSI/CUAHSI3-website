The New Mexico Environment Department's Drinking Water Bureau is hiring a scientist to support source water protection planning and drinking water safety for public water systems across New Mexico. The role involves developing Source Water Protection Plans and Assessments, collecting and analyzing water quality samples, and using GIS software for data analysis and contaminant inventories. A bachelor's degree in a relevant science or engineering field plus two years of experience, or six years of combined education and experience, is required.
