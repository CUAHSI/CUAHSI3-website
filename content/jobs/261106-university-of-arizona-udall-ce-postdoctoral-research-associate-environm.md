The Udall Center for Studies in Public Policy at the University of Arizona is hiring a two-year postdoctoral researcher to conduct applied research on environmental governance topics including water governance, PFAS policy, and co-stewardship. The position involves designing and implementing research, mentoring students, and collaborating with an interdisciplinary faculty team. Applicants must hold a PhD in geography, environmental policy, political science, or a related field.
