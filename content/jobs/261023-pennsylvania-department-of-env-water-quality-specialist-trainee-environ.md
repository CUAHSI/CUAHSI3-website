The Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection is hiring a Water Quality Specialist Trainee in its Oil and Gas division to assist with water quality inspections, complaint investigations, environmental sampling, and compliance documentation. The role provides structured on-the-job training under experienced staff. Candidates must have a bachelor's degree or four years of experience in environmental science, geology, biology, or a closely related field.
