The U.S. Geological Survey's Geology, Energy & Minerals Science Center is hiring a Hydrologist at the GS-12 or GS-13 level in Reston, VA. The role involves conducting fundamental and applied research in subsurface hydrology, multiphase flow, and numerical modeling of fluid flow in geologic formations. Candidates must meet basic educational requirements in physical or natural science and have specialized experience in subsurface hydrology or multiphase flow through porous media equivalent to the GS-11 or GS-12 level.
