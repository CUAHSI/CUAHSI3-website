The Bureau of Reclamation is hiring a Natural Resources Specialist under the Pathways Recent Graduate Program at the GS-07 level. The role involves supporting environmental compliance, natural resource monitoring, wildlife habitat management, and restoration projects. Applicants must have completed a qualifying degree in biological sciences, natural resource management, or a related field within the past two years and meet education or specialized experience requirements.
