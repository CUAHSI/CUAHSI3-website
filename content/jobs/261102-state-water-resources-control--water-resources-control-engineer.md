The California State Water Resources Control Board's Division of Drinking Water is hiring a Water Resources Control Engineer for its Santa Rosa office. The role involves overseeing public water systems to ensure safe drinking water for Californians. Candidates must be eligible under California civil service requirements and possess a valid driver's license.
