LSU's Department of Geology and Geophysics is hiring a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Hydrogeophysics. The role requires a Ph.D. in Hydrology, Hydrogeology, Geophysics, Earth Science, or a related field, with expertise spanning water-related geophysics or hydrogeology using geophysical methods. The successful candidate is expected to build an independent, externally funded research program and contribute to undergraduate and graduate teaching.
