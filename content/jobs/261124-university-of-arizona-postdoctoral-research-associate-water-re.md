The University of Arizona Water Research Accelerator is hiring a Postdoctoral Research Associate to coordinate interdisciplinary water research, support collaborative proposal development, and advance convergence science across university departments and research groups. The role requires a PhD in hydrology, water resources, environmental science, or a closely related field. The successful candidate must have a strong technical water science background combined with demonstrated ability to work across disciplinary boundaries.
