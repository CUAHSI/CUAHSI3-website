The Bureau of Reclamation is hiring a Hydrologic Technician (GS-05/06) for its Blythe Hydrographic Team in California. The role involves collecting and analyzing hydrologic data at river, canal, and drain gauging stations, including discharge measurements, water sampling, and data computations. Applicants must be U.S. citizens with specialized experience in gaging station operations or a qualifying bachelor's degree in a relevant science or engineering field.
