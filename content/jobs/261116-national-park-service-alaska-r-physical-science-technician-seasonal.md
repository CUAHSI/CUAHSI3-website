The National Park Service Alaska Region is hiring seasonal Physical Science Technicians at GS-05 through GS-07 grade levels across multiple Alaska park units. The positions involve field and laboratory work including environmental data collection, monitoring equipment installation, and sample processing in support of air, water, and resource management programs. Applicants must have experience installing environmental monitoring instrumentation and data collection equipment.
