The University of Minnesota Department of Soil, Water, and Climate is hiring a Postdoctoral Researcher to develop knowledge-guided machine learning models for river forecasting and water resources decision-support across the Upper Mississippi River Basin under a NASA-funded project. The role involves building and evaluating KGML architectures integrating NASA Earth Observations, leading peer-reviewed publications, and collaborating with federal and regional water management partners. A PhD in a relevant quantitative field and demonstrated publication record are required, with strong preference for experience in deep learning and large-scale hydrology.
