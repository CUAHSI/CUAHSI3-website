The USGS Wyoming-Montana Water Science Center is hiring a GS-12 Field Office Chief to supervise hydrologic technicians and oversee surface-water, groundwater, water-quality, and sediment monitoring programs in the Billings area. The role involves managing data collection operations, ensuring compliance with USGS standards, and coordinating with Federal, State, Tribal, and local partners. Applicants must have one year of specialized hydrologic experience equivalent to the GS-11 level and a valid driver's license.
