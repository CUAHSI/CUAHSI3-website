The Minnesota Department of Natural Resources is hiring a full-time Watershed Specialist in the Division of Ecological and Water Resources. The role involves conducting watershed assessments using river ecology principles, providing technical assistance on water quality impairments, and supporting the Clean Water Legacy initiative in the Central Region. Applicants must have a bachelor's degree in a related field and at least three years of professional experience in natural resources management pertaining to lakes, rivers, or watersheds.
