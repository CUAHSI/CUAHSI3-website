The National Park Service is hiring a GS-13 Marine Ecologist at Everglades National Park to lead coastal, estuarine, and marine ecology research, monitoring, and restoration programs supporting Everglades restoration and resource management. The role involves supervising scientific staff, advising on marine resource management, and coordinating with partner agencies and universities. Candidates must have a biology-related degree with specific coursework in ecology and physical/mathematical sciences, plus at least one year of specialized experience in coastal, estuarine, or marine ecology at the GS-12 equivalent level.
