NEIWPCC is hiring a full-time Information Officer to support Connecticut's Water Monitoring and Assessment Program through data management, tool development, and analysis to meet state and federal reporting requirements. The position is funded for two years and based at the CT Department of Energy & Environmental Protection office. Candidates should have a bachelor's degree in a relevant field and experience with SQL, Python, R, and database management.
