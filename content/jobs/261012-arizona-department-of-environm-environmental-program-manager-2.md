ADEQ is hiring a unit manager for its Standards & Assessment Unit within the Water Quality Division to oversee surface water quality standards and Clean Water Act compliance programs. The role involves supervising a team of technical experts, managing budgets and grants, and ensuring regulatory compliance. Candidates should have a relevant environmental science degree and 3-5 years of regulatory experience.
