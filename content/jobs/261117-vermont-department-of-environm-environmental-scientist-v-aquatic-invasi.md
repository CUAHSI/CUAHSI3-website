The Vermont Department of Environmental Conservation's Lakes and Ponds Program is hiring a full-time Environmental Scientist V to coordinate statewide aquatic invasive species prevention and management programs, administer grants, and provide technical assistance and outreach. The role also supports inland lake cyanobacteria monitoring and aquatic nuisance control. A master's degree in a relevant natural science with 3 years of experience, or a bachelor's degree with 5 years of experience, is required.
