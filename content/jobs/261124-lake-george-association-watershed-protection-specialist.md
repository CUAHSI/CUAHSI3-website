The Lake George Association is hiring a full-time Watershed Protection Specialist to assess land-use activities, investigate watershed concerns, and support water-quality advocacy and science for Lake George and its watershed. The role reports to the Lake George Waterkeeper and involves field assessments, GIS analysis, regulatory review, and community engagement. A background in environmental science, planning, or engineering with at least two years of relevant experience and GIS proficiency is preferred.
