The University of Pennsylvania's Water Center is hiring a Research Specialist to lead applied research and policy initiatives related to water infrastructure, emerging contaminants, and water equity. The role involves designing research projects, analyzing qualitative and quantitative data, and translating findings into communications for stakeholders including utilities, government agencies, and nonprofits. A bachelor's degree with 3–5 years of relevant experience is required; a master's degree in environmental policy, water resources, or a related field is preferred.
