The Nelson Institute for Environmental Studies and the Department of Population Health Sciences at UW-Madison are hiring a joint-appointment, tenure-track environmental health scientist at the Assistant or Associate Professor level. The position is part of the Wisconsin RISE-EARTH initiative and requires a PhD or MD/Master's with expertise in areas such as air pollution epidemiology, drinking water contaminants, biomarker-based exposure assessment, or exposome research. The successful candidate will develop an extramurally funded research program, teach graduate and undergraduate courses, and mentor students across relevant degree programs.
