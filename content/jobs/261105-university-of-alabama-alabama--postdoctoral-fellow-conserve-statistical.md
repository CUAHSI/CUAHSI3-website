The University of Alabama's Alabama Water Institute is hiring a Postdoctoral Fellow to work with the CONSERVE Research Group and USDA Agricultural Research Service partners on agricultural water use and watershed health. The role involves developing statistical, geospatial, and AI-assisted models for aquifer recharge, contaminant transport, and groundwater decision-support tools. A PhD in hydrology, hydrogeology, civil/environmental engineering, geography, or a closely related field is required, along with strong skills in statistical modeling and proficiency in R and/or Python.
