NRDC is hiring a graduate intern to support its Freshwater Ecosystems team within the Nature Department's Lands Division in Washington, DC. The intern will conduct policy research and analysis on aquatic nature-based solutions and drinking water utility investment in freshwater ecosystem restoration in the West and Midwest. Applicants must be currently enrolled in a graduate program with strong research, writing, and communication skills.
