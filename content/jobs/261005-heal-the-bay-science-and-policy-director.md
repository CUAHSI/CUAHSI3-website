Heal the Bay, a Southern California environmental nonprofit, is hiring a Science and Policy Director to lead its science, policy, and advocacy efforts on water quality and coastal resources. The role involves managing a team of scientists and policy experts, developing advocacy campaigns, and engaging with policymakers in Los Angeles and Sacramento. Candidates should have 10+ years in environmental policy or related fields, an advanced degree, and at least five years of team leadership experience.
