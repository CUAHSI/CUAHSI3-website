Heal the Bay, a Southern California environmental nonprofit, is hiring a Science and Policy Director to lead its science, policy, and advocacy efforts on water quality and coastal resources. Candidates should have 10+ years in environmental policy or related fields, an advanced degree, and at least five years of team leadership experience.
