Cornell University's Watershed Biogeochemistry in the Anthropocene Lab is hiring a Postdoctoral Associate to investigate mercury-selenium antagonism through fieldwork in Peru and mesocosm experiments at Cornell. The role involves biogeochemical field campaigns, laboratory analyses (including synchrotron-based methods), and data analysis leading to peer-reviewed publications. Candidates must hold a Ph.D. in a relevant field and have demonstrated experience in biogeochemical lab and field research.
