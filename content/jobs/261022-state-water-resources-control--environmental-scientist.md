The California State Water Resources Control Board's Division of Drinking Water is hiring an Environmental Scientist to serve as a laboratory assessor in the Environmental Laboratory Accreditation Program (ELAP). The role involves assessing and enhancing laboratory capabilities across the state to support environmental monitoring and protection. Candidates must apply through CalCareers and reside in California upon appointment.
