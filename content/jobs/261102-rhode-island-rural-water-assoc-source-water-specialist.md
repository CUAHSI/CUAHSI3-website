Rhode Island Rural Water Association is hiring a Source Water Specialist to support protection and sustainability of drinking water resources through assessments, protection planning, and technical assistance. The role involves collaborating with public water systems, municipalities, and regulatory agencies to identify risks and develop strategies for water quality and quantity. An associate or bachelor's degree in environmental science, civil engineering, or a related field is preferred, along with relevant experience in source water protection or water resources.
