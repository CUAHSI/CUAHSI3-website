The Washington Department of Ecology's Environmental Assessment Program is hiring a Staff Scientist to support environmental data management, toxics water quality assessments, and database coordination in the Toxics Studies Unit. The role involves data entry, quality control, staff training on the EIM system, and assisting with toxics assessments in water and tissue. Candidates need 7 years of combined experience and/or education in natural science or data management.
