The Arizona Department of Water Resources is hiring a Water Resources Specialist 3 to join its Assured and Adequate Water Supply Section. The role involves processing and analyzing applications for Designations of Assured Water Supply, reviewing hydrologic studies and legal documentation, and preparing regulatory determinations. Candidates should have at least seven years of professional water resource management experience, or a relevant degree with commensurate experience.
