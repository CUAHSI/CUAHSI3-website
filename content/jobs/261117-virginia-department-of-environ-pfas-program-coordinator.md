Virginia DEQ is hiring a PFAS Program Coordinator to manage and coordinate the agency's PFAS water programs through project management, policy development, and technical support. The role involves developing PFAS policies and regulations, ensuring program compliance, and providing guidance to staff and stakeholders. Candidates should have education or experience in environmental science or engineering and demonstrated knowledge of Clean Water Act and state water program processes.
