The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers St. Paul District is hiring a senior Hydrologic Technician (GS-10/11) to lead data collection activities supporting engineers and hydrologists. Responsibilities include installing and maintaining stream and rain gages, performing discharge measurements, managing the district's hydrologic gage network, and supporting GIS and water management studies. Applicants must have one year of specialized experience in hydrologic/hydraulic data analysis, field equipment maintenance, and technical documentation, and must qualify under specific federal employment authorities.
