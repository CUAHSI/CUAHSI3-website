The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation is hiring an Environmental Program Specialist (or Trainee) for its Division of Water in Region 1. The role involves implementing water discharge, stormwater, coastal, and floodplain management programs, including inspections, compliance, enforcement, and technical assistance. Candidates must meet education and experience requirements ranging from four to six years (with degree substitutions allowed) depending on the level applied for.
