Northwestern University's Department of Earth and Environmental Sciences (DEEPS) is hiring a full-time tenure-track associate or full professor in environmental science with an emphasis on water in the environment. The position is based in Evanston, Illinois and offers a salary range of $125,000–$225,000 per year plus benefits. Candidates should have expertise relevant to water-focused environmental science research and teaching.
