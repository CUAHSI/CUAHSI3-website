The University of Alabama's Alabama Water Institute is hiring a Postdoctoral Fellow to work with the CONSERVE Research Group and USDA ARS partners on agricultural water use and watershed health. The role involves developing statistical, geospatial, and AI-assisted models for aquifer recharge, contaminant transport, and nature-based runoff control, and publishing results in peer-reviewed journals. Applicants must hold a PhD in hydrology, hydrogeology, civil/environmental engineering, geography, or a closely related field.
