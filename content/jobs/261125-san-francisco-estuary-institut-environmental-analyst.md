SFEI is hiring an entry-level Environmental Analyst to support the San Francisco Bay Nutrient Management Strategy's water quality monitoring program. The role involves maintaining a network of water quality sensors, conducting boat-based fieldwork, and performing data QA/QC and analysis. A Bachelor's degree in environmental science, engineering, oceanography, or a related field is required.
