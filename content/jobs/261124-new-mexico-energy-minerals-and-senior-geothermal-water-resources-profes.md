The New Mexico Energy Conservation and Management Division is hiring a senior professional to administer the state's geothermal resources program, including permitting wells, drafting regulations, managing grants and loans, and providing technical assistance. The role requires a bachelor's degree in a relevant natural or physical science or engineering field plus four years of experience, or eight years of combined education and experience. Key qualifications include project management, data analysis, and experience in natural resources or environmental sectors.
