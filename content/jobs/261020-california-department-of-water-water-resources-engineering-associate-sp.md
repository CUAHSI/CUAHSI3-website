The California Department of Water Resources is hiring a Water Resources Engineering Associate (Specialist) for its Southern Field Division in Castaic. The role involves complex engineering and operations duties related to water transport, delivery, water sales, and power generation. Candidates must meet the Water Resources Engineering Associate (Specialist) class specification minimum qualifications and hold a valid California Class C driver's license.
