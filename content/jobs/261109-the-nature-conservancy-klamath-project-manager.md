The Nature Conservancy is hiring a two-year term Project Manager to develop and implement streamflow, agricultural water management, and riparian restoration projects in the Shasta and Scott River watersheds of the Klamath Basin. The role involves collaborating with farmers, ranchers, irrigation districts, tribes, and agencies to advance voluntary water management strategies that improve instream flows while supporting agricultural viability. Candidates need a BA/BS degree and at least 5 years of experience in conservation practice, including project management, partnership development, and habitat restoration.
