The University of Glasgow is recruiting a PhD student supervised by a multi-institution team to develop statistical and ML/AI methods for uncertainty quantification and communication in environmental pollution models. The project focuses on air and water quality applications, integrating diverse observational data with approaches such as physics-informed surrogate models, conformal prediction, and LLMs. Candidates should have a background in statistics, machine learning, or a related quantitative field with interest in environmental systems.
