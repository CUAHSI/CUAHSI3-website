The Washington Department of Ecology's Office of Chehalis Basin is hiring a Flood Risk Reduction Coordinator to oversee home elevation programs, provide floodplain management policy guidance, and coordinate with local, Tribal, state, and federal partners on flood risk reduction. The position requires nine years of experience and/or education in environmental or land use planning, including expertise in floodplain management, regulatory compliance, and program/project management. A Certified Floodplain Manager credential and experience with FEMA programs and public works contracting are desired.
