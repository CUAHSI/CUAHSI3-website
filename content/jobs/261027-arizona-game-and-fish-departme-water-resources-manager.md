The Arizona Game and Fish Department is hiring a Water Resources Manager in Phoenix to conduct water rights investigations, research, and management for properties including wildlife areas, fish hatcheries, and shooting ranges. The role sits within the Wildlife Management Division's Habitat Branch and reports to the Land and Water Program Supervisor. Candidates should have a background in hydrology, water law, or water resources management along with knowledge of Arizona water rights law and GIS mapping.
