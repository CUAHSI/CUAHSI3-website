The University of Nevada, Reno is hiring a Research Technician to support the Nevada Forest and Woodland Restoration Institute in studying linkages among forest fuels treatments, catchment hydrology, and ecosystem processes in the northern Sierra Nevada. The role involves leading field crews, collecting ecological and hydrological data, processing laboratory samples, and assisting with data management and analysis. A bachelor's degree in a relevant biological or environmental science field (or equivalent experience) and a valid driver's license are required.
