The Bureau of Reclamation's Bay Delta Office is hiring a Civil Engineer at the GS-11/12 level to develop and apply hydrologic and water-operations models (e.g., CalSim/WRIMS, CalLite) supporting planning and operational studies for the Central Valley Project and State Water Project. The role involves scripting (Python, R, SQL), scenario analysis, technical documentation, and coordination with internal and external partners. Candidates must hold an accredited engineering degree (or equivalent) and have at least one year of specialized experience in hydrologic or hydraulic modeling, water management operations, or related water-resource engineering.
