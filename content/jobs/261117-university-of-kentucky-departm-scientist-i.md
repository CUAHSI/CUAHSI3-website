The University of Kentucky's Department of Plant and Soil Sciences is hiring a full-time Field Scientist (Scientist I / Water Quality Technician) to support a USDA NRCS-funded wetland water quality research project in western Kentucky. The role involves operating water-quality monitoring equipment, collecting environmental samples, managing data, and assisting with technical reporting at wetland field sites. A Master's degree in agriculture, soil, water, geology, or an environmental discipline is required.
