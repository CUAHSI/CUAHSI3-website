NOAA's National Weather Service is hiring a GS-13 Meteorologist, Hydrologist, or Physical Scientist to serve as an Emergency Response Specialist at one of its Sector Operations Centers across the U.S. The role involves providing impact-based decision support services to emergency managers and decision makers during high-impact weather, water, or environmental events. Applicants must meet basic education requirements in meteorology, hydrology, or physical science and have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-12, including weather or environmental data analysis in support of emergency response.
