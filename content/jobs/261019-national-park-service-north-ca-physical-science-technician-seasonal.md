The National Park Service is hiring seasonal Physical Science Technicians at the GS-06 or GS-07 level to support physical science monitoring and resource management programs at North Cascades National Park. Duties include field data collection, sample processing, equipment operation, and GIS support for air, water, and environmental contamination programs. Applicants must have relevant specialized experience or graduate education in a physical science field, and the expected entry on duty is April 2027.
