NRDC is hiring a legal intern for its Environmental Health Department's Air & Water Division for a 10-week paid summer session (May 24–July 30, 2027). The intern will support projects on drinking water protection from lead, PFAS, and other toxic chemicals, including congressional legislation and likely litigation against EPA rollbacks. Applicants must be currently enrolled in a JD or LLM program and familiar with Westlaw legal research.
