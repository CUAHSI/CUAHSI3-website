Utah State University is hiring an endowed chair (Associate or Full Professor) to serve as Director of the Center for Colorado River Studies within the Janet Quinney Lawson Institute for Land, Water, and Air. The role involves research, teaching, outreach, and stakeholder engagement focused on the Colorado River Basin. Candidates must hold a Ph.D. or J.D. in a relevant field and have demonstrated national or international impact in water science, policy, governance, or environmental management.
