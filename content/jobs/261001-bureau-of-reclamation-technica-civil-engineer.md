The Bureau of Reclamation's Technical Service Center is hiring a GS-13 Civil Engineer to lead and manage complex engineering projects involving water storage, conveyance, hydropower, and related systems. The role involves directing technical teams, conducting engineering analyses, and preparing project management and design documentation. Applicants must have an accredited engineering degree or equivalent and at least one year of specialized experience at the GS-12 level.
