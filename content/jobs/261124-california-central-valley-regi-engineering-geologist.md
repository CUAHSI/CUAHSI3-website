The California Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board's Redding Office is hiring an Engineering Geologist for its Forest Activities Program. The role involves evaluating, inspecting, monitoring, and regulating timberland management activities on non-federal lands to protect water quality in accordance with the California Water Code and CEQA. Candidates must be licensed Engineering Geologists with relevant geologic and hydrogeologic assessment skills and the ability to perform fieldwork.
