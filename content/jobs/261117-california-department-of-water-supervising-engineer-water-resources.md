The California Department of Water Resources is hiring a Supervising Engineer (Specialist) to lead water planning activities within the Division of Planning, Plan Formulation Branch. The role involves guiding multi-disciplinary planning studies at regional and watershed scales, collaborating with diverse stakeholders, and representing DWR at public forums in support of the California Water Plan. Candidates must meet the minimum qualifications for the Supervising Engineer Water Resources classification and have experience in multi-sector water management planning.
