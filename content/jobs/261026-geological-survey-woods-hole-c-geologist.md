The U.S. Geological Survey's Woods Hole Coastal & Marine Science Center is hiring a Geologist at the GS-09 or GS-11 level to collect, analyze, and interpret marine geological and geophysical data, including seismic-reflection data, using seafloor mapping technologies and geophysical software. The role involves conducting field work aboard research vessels, integrating geologic and geophysical datasets, and preparing scientific reports and publications. Applicants must hold a geology degree with supplemental coursework in related sciences and have specialized experience or graduate education equivalent to the target grade level.
