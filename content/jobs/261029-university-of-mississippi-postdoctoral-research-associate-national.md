The University of Mississippi's NCCHE is hiring a postdoctoral researcher to develop and apply advanced numerical models for watershed hydrology, surface water systems, flooding, and water quality. The role involves model calibration, validation, scientific programming, and supporting computational tools for flood forecasting and water resources management. Applicants must hold a PhD in Civil/Environmental Engineering, Environmental Science, or a closely related field.
