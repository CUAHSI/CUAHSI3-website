IDEM is hiring an Environmental Manager 2 to serve as a project manager and specialist in aquatic ecology within the Watershed Assessment and Planning Branch. The role involves field sampling of water chemistry, E. coli, and algae from rivers, streams, and lakes, along with data management, technical analysis, and reporting. Candidates need at least five years of environmental experience with skills in ArcGIS, R programming, and environmental sampling methodology.
