The University of Pittsburgh's Pittsburgh Water Collaboratory is hiring a full-time analyst to support environmental water research, spatial and statistical analysis, field sampling, and community science capacity building. The role requires a bachelor's degree, at least 3 years of experience, and proficiency with GIS and programming tools such as R, Python, or MATLAB. Physical fieldwork including wading in streams and carrying equipment is required.
