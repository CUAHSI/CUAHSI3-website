The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers is hiring a GS-13 team lead for its Water Control Data System (WCDS), responsible for managing hydrologic and meteorologic data and supporting water management missions across districts. The role involves leading a team, coordinating with contractors, preparing procurement specifications, and overseeing hydro-meteorological data networks. Applicants must have a qualifying engineering or hydrology degree and at least one year of specialized experience at the GS-12 level involving hydrologic/meteorologic data systems and procurement specifications.
