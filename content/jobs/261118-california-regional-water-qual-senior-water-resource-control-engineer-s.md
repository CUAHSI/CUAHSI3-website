The California Regional Water Quality Control Board, San Diego Region is hiring a Senior Water Resource Control Engineer to lead the Source Control Regulation Unit. The role involves administering and enforcing NPDES permit programs for wastewater and stormwater discharges. Candidates must meet civil service eligibility requirements for the classification and possess a valid engineer's license or equivalent qualifications.
