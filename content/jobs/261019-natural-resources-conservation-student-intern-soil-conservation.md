NRCS is hiring student interns under the Pathways Internship Program to assist with soil and water conservation planning, field surveys, and conservation practice layout. Applicants must be enrolled at least half-time in an accredited institution pursuing a degree in agriculture, soil science, natural resources, or a related field. Positions range from GS-02 to GS-04 depending on education level, with potential non-competitive conversion to a permanent GS-0457 position upon meeting program requirements.
