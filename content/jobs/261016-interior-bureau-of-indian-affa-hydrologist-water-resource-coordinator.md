The Bureau of Indian Affairs is hiring a GS-13 Hydrologist to coordinate water resource and water rights work in support of tribal water rights claims and hydrologic studies across multiple regional offices. The role involves developing hydrologic models, analyzing water resource data, advising on water rights policy, and communicating findings to tribes, governments, and stakeholders. Applicants must have a qualifying degree in hydrology or related physical/natural science with required calculus and physics coursework, plus one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-12.
