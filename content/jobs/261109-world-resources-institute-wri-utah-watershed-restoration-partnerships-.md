WRI's U.S. Forests and Water program is hiring a manager to advance watershed restoration, partnerships, and resilience projects across Utah, with an initial focus on the Jordan River watershed and Greater Salt Lake Basin. The role involves coordinating partners, securing funding, navigating permitting, and delivering on-the-ground restoration outcomes. Candidates need at least 7 years of relevant experience, an established professional network in Utah, and familiarity with Utah water policy, watershed planning, and wildfire resilience.
