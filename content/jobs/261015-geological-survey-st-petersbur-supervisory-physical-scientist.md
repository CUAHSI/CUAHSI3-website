USGS St. Petersburg Coastal & Marine Science Center is hiring a GS-13 Supervisory Physical Scientist to lead numerical modeling of coastal change hazards and supervise modeling staff. The role involves designing interdisciplinary coastal studies, developing hydrodynamic models, and representing the center at technical meetings. Candidates must have a physical science degree with 24 semester hours in physical science and at least one year of specialized experience at the GS-12 level involving coastal numerical modeling.
