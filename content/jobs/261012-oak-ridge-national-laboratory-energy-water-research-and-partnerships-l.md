Oak Ridge National Laboratory is hiring a senior Energy-Water Research and Partnerships Lead to provide scientific and strategic leadership at the intersection of energy and water systems. The role involves growing an externally funded technical portfolio, building partnerships across government, industry, utilities, and academia, and leading multidisciplinary teams on complex energy-water challenges. Candidates must hold an M.S. in a relevant discipline with at least 16 years of technically focused experience in energy-water systems or a closely related field.
