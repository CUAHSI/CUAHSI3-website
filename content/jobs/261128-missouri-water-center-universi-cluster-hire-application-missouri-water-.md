The Missouri Water Center is hiring two postdoctoral researchers and two PhD students for interdisciplinary water resources research on the Lower Missouri River. Positions focus on river hydraulic modeling and digital twins (funded by NIST) and PFAS/contaminant fate and transport in river and wetland systems (funded by USGS). Candidates should have relevant PhDs or BS/MS degrees with experience in hydraulic modeling, CFD, LC-MS/MS, or environmental chemistry depending on the role.
