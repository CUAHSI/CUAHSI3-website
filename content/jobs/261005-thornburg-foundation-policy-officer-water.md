The Thornburg Foundation is hiring a Policy Officer to lead its Water Initiative, managing a multi-year grantmaking portfolio focused on modernizing water management in New Mexico. The role involves developing policy strategies, building coalitions with diverse stakeholders including Tribal and Hispanic communities, and coordinating with grantees and partners. Candidates must have 5+ years of relevant political or advocacy experience and an undergraduate degree in a related field, with a graduate degree preferred.
