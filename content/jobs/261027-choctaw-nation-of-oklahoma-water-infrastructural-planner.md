The Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma is hiring a Water Infrastructural Planner to support drinking water and wastewater infrastructure across tribal facilities through technical assessments, funding coordination, and planning. The role requires a bachelor's degree in an engineering-based field or equivalent experience, plus at least 5 years of treatment plant operator experience. The position is full-time, on-site, and reports to the Sustainable Communities Manager.
