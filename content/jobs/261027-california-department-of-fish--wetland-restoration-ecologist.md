The California Department of Fish and Wildlife's Fish Restoration Program is hiring two Senior Environmental Scientists (Specialists) to conduct ecological analysis, fieldwork, and interagency coordination for tidal wetland restoration across the upper San Francisco Estuary. Responsibilities include leading restoration effectiveness analyses, synthesizing findings into technical reports, mentoring colleagues, and conducting boat-based biological sampling. Candidates must meet the Senior Environmental Scientist (Specialist) classification requirements and be able to lift 40 pounds and swim 50 yards.
