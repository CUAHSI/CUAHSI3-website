HydroSolutions, Inc. is hiring a Water Rights Specialist at junior or mid-level to provide technical consulting on Montana water right permitting, water right changes, and Water Court Adjudication proceedings. Responsibilities include preparing DNRC applications, conducting historical water use investigations, and performing hydrologic analyses. Candidates need at least one year of relevant experience (five for mid-level), an undergraduate degree in a related field, and familiarity with Montana water rights administration.
