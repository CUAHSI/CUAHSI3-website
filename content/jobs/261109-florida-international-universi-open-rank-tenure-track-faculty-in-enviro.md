The Department of Earth and Environment at Florida International University is hiring a full-time, open-rank, tenure-track faculty member in Environmental Geochemistry to begin August 2027. The position involves developing an externally funded research program, teaching undergraduate and graduate courses, and mentoring students. A Ph.D. in Environmental Geochemistry, Geology, Environmental Science, Environmental Engineering, or a closely related field is required by the start date.
