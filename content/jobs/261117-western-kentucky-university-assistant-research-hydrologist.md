Western Kentucky University is hiring an Assistant Research Hydrologist to serve as Lab Manager for the Crawford Hydrology Laboratory, which specializes in groundwater flow and karst aquifer investigations using fluorescent dye tracing. The role involves managing daily lab operations, overseeing quality assurance, supervising student employees, and assisting with field investigations. A Master's degree in Geosciences, Hydrology, Environmental Science, or a related STEM field is required along with demonstrated analytical laboratory experience.
