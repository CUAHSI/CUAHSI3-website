The Minnesota DNR is hiring a State Program Administrator Director to lead statewide stream habitat improvement and salmonid management programs based in Brainerd. The role involves supervising staff, managing state and federal funding, overseeing dam removal and habitat projects, and coordinating with partner agencies. A bachelor's degree in biology or fisheries plus four years of professional fisheries experience are required.
