The New Mexico Office of the State Engineer is hiring a Water Resources Supervisor to lead staff in the Hydrology Bureau and perform complex hydrologic, hydrogeologic, and engineering analyses supporting water rights administration, adjudications, and interstate stream compact compliance. The role involves supervising three Water Resources Professionals, managing hydrologic models, and providing expert testimony in litigation. A bachelor's degree in a water resources-related field plus six years of relevant experience is required, or ten years of combined education and experience.
