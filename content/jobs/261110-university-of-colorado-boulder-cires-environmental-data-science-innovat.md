ESIIL within CIRES at the University of Colorado Boulder is hiring Postdoctoral Associates to conduct environmental data science research addressing pressing environmental challenges. The two-year, primarily in-person position involves developing open-data, cloud-computing, and AI-based research projects in environmental biology. Applicants must hold a Ph.D. in an environmental, biological, social, computer, or data science field by the start date.
