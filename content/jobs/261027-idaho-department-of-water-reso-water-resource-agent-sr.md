The Idaho Department of Water Resources is hiring a Water Resource Agent or Senior Water Resource Agent in Boise to support the Water Supply Bank program. The role involves reviewing water right applications, maintaining water right records, and assisting the public with water law questions. Minimum qualifications include knowledge of hydrology and experience interpreting public land survey legal descriptions; senior level requires additional GIS and regulatory experience.
