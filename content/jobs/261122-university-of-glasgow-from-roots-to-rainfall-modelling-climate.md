The University of Glasgow is recruiting a PhD student supervised by researchers from Glasgow, Lancaster, and Edinburgh to study groundwater-dependent ecosystems (GDEs) and their interactions with climate. The project involves coupling vegetation and groundwater models, atmospheric moisture tracking, and GPU-accelerated computing to quantify how GDEs influence regional rainfall and assess their vulnerability to groundwater depletion. Candidates should have a background relevant to hydrology, ecohydrology, or related Earth/environmental sciences with interest in computational modelling.
