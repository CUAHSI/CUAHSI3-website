The University of Alabama's Global Water Security Center is hiring a Data Scientist I to assist with data management, integration, and analysis of water, weather, climate, and environmental datasets. The role involves ETL processes, database coordination, statistical analysis, and data visualization in support of national water and environmental security research. A bachelor's degree in a relevant field, experience with large environmental datasets, and U.S. citizenship or permanent residency are required.
