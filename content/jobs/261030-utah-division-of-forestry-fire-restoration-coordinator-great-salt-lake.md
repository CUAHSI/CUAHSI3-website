The Utah Division of Forestry, Fire and State Lands, in partnership with the Division of Wildlife Resources, is hiring a full-time Restoration Coordinator to manage vegetation restoration and wetland management projects in Great Salt Lake wetlands. Responsibilities include planning and tracking treatments with ArcGIS, operating airboats and heavy equipment, managing budgets and contracts, and collaborating with researchers and partners. Minimum qualifications include at least one year of professional plant identification experience, technical writing skills, and knowledge of Great Salt Lake wetland plants and invasive species management.
