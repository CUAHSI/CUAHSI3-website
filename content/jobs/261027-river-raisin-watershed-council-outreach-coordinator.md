The River Raisin Watershed Council is hiring a full-time Outreach Coordinator to lead community education, environmental outreach events, and field program coordination. The role involves delivering watershed-related presentations to schools and community groups, managing volunteers, and coordinating water sampling and nature programs. A bachelor's degree in environmental science, education, or a related field (or equivalent experience) is required, along with strong public speaking skills and comfort with outdoor fieldwork.
