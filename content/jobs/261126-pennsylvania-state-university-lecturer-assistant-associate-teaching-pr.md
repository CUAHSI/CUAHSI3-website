Penn State's Department of Energy and Mineral Engineering seeks a full-time, non-tenure track faculty member to teach undergraduate and graduate courses in environmental systems engineering topics such as air and water pollution control, environmental chemistry, and risk assessment. The position is a 9-month academic appointment funded for three years with renewal possibility, anticipated to start Summer 2027. Candidates must hold a doctoral degree (for Teaching Professor ranks) or an MS with significant industry or government experience (for Lecturer or Professor of Practice ranks) in environmental engineering or a related field.
