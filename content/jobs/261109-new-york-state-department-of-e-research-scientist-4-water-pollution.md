The New York State DEC Division of Environmental Remediation is hiring a Research Scientist 4 to supervise the Research and Data Analytics Section within the Special Projects Bureau, overseeing research on hazardous waste cleanup including PCBs, PFAS, and other emerging contaminants. The role involves managing environmental data systems, directing research staff, and coordinating academic partnerships. Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree plus five years of professional research experience in water pollution or a related environmental or scientific field.
