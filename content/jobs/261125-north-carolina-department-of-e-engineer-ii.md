The NC Department of Environmental Quality is hiring an Engineer II to serve as team lead in the Public Water Supply Section's Compliance Services Branch, overseeing compliance with the Revised Total Coliform Rule, Ground Water Rule, and Nitrate/Nitrite Requirements. The role involves directing team members, interpreting drinking water regulations, and advising water system stakeholders on compliance matters. A bachelor's degree in engineering and three years of progressively responsible experience are required.
