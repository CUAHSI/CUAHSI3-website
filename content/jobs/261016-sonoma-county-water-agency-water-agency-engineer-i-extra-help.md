Sonoma County Water Agency is hiring a temporary extra-help Water Agency Engineer I to support water resources engineering work in its Environmental Resources and Engineering Divisions. Responsibilities include hydrologic data analysis, water supply planning, surface and groundwater sampling, and hydraulic modeling for the Russian River watershed. A four-year engineering degree or Engineer-in-Training certificate is required; graduate-level experience or a graduate degree in water resources engineering or hydrologic sciences is highly desirable.
