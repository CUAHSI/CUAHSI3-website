The Arizona Department of Water Resources is hiring a Deputy Assistant Director to manage and oversee regulatory, permitting, and planning sections within its Water Planning and Permitting Division. The role involves senior leadership responsibilities including policy development, stakeholder engagement, staff supervision, and representing the agency on water resource management issues. Candidates should have at least 10 years of professional water resource management experience, or a relevant degree with proportionally fewer years of experience, along with demonstrated managerial and government experience.
