The Washington State Department of Ecology's Water Quality Program is hiring a Hydrogeologist 3 to provide groundwater and hydrological technical services, administer the underground injection control (UIC) program, and serve as the program expert on state groundwater standards regulations. The position requires a valid Washington State Geologist License and Hydrogeologist Specialty License along with at least seven years of relevant experience or equivalent education. The role is based at the Lacey, WA headquarters with telework options available after an initial training period.
