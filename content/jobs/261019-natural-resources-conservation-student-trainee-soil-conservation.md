The Natural Resources Conservation Service is hiring student interns under the Pathways Student Internship Program for paid, career-oriented work in soil conservation and natural resource management. Interns assist with conservation planning, field surveys, and on-the-job training across soil and water practices. Applicants must be enrolled at least half-time in an accredited institution pursuing a degree in agriculture, soil science, natural resources, or a related field, with a minimum GPA of 2.5.
