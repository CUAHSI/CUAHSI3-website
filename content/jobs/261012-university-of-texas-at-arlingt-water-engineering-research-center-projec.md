The University of Texas at Arlington's Civil Engineering Department is hiring a Project Manager to support the Water Engineering Research Center (WERC) by managing project budgets, coordinating research studies, and helping secure funding. The role requires a bachelor's degree in engineering, a Texas PE license, and 10 years of experience in green stormwater infrastructure, nature-based solutions, and weather resilience. The position is grant-funded through August 31, 2027.
