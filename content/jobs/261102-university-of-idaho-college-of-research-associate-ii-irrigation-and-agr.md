The University of Idaho's College of Agricultural and Life Sciences is hiring a Research Associate II to conduct field-based research on irrigation and agricultural water management at its Kimberly location. The role involves planning and executing scientific studies, analyzing data, and preparing publications under general supervision. A Master's degree in Agronomy, Soil Science, Agricultural Engineering, or a related field plus two years of post-degree research experience are required.
