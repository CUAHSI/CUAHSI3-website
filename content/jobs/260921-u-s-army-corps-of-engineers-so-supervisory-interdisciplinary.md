The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Sacramento District is hiring a Supervisory Interdisciplinary position within the Civil Works Design Branch. The role involves supervising up to six Engineering Technical Leads and administrative personnel, overseeing engineering project management for investigation, study, design, and construction of facilities. Applicants must be U.S. citizens with a qualifying degree or combination of education and experience in Civil Engineering, Hydrology, or Geology.
