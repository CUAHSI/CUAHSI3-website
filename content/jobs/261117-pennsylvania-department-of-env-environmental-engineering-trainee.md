The Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection is hiring a full-time Environmental Engineering Trainee for its Northwest Regional Office, Safe Drinking Water Program. The role involves reviewing permit applications, conducting field inspections of public water supply facilities, and providing technical assistance under a formal training program. Applicants must have a bachelor's degree in engineering or one year of engineering experience with an active Engineering Intern certificate, plus a valid motor vehicle license.
