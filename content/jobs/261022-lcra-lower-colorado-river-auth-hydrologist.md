LCRA is hiring a Hydrologist for its River Operations Center to manage the lower Colorado River during drought and flood conditions. The role involves daily water supply calculations, flood monitoring, and supporting release decisions using RiverWare and Excel models. Candidates need four or more years of hydrology experience or a relevant degree, along with knowledge of reservoir systems and dam operations.
