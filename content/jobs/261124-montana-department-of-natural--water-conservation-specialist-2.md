The Montana Department of Natural Resources & Conservation is hiring a Water Conservation Specialist 2 in Lewistown to evaluate water right applications, conduct field investigations, and investigate water use issues. The role involves technical analysis, public outreach, and ensuring water is managed in accordance with Montana water law. A bachelor's degree in natural resources or a related field plus three years of water resources experience is required.
