Auburn University's Department of Crop, Soil, and Environmental Sciences is hiring a Postdoctoral Fellow for a two-year (renewable up to four years) position focused on phosphorus transport, nutrient management, and water quality research. The role involves field and laboratory studies, rainfall simulation experiments, edge-of-field monitoring, and application of models such as APEX and SWAT. A Ph.D. in soil science, hydrology, or a closely related field is required, along with expertise in GIS, remote sensing, and a demonstrated publication record.
