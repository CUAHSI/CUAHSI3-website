The National Park Service is hiring a temporary Ecologist at Yosemite National Park to support vegetation monitoring and management programs, including bare soil assessment and riparian vegetation analysis in Poopenaut Valley. The position involves analyzing complex vegetation datasets, applying remote sensing and GIS tools, and preparing manuscripts for publication. Candidates must have a qualifying degree in biology or ecology plus at least one year of specialized experience at the GS-09 level or equivalent graduate education.
