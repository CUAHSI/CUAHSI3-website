The Colorado Division of Water Resources is hiring a Water Commissioner for District 4 (Thompson River Basins) to administer and distribute water under Colorado water law, enforce state water rights, measure diversions, and assist the public with water-related matters. The position requires six years of experience with water measurement, control, or distribution work, plus a high school diploma or GED. Applicants must be current Colorado residents.
