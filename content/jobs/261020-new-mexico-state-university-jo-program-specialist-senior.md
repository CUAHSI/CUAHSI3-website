NMSU's Jornada Basin Long-Term Ecological Research Program is hiring a Program Specialist Senior for a one-year, funding-contingent appointment. The role involves conducting field and laboratory ecological measurements, managing data entry and documentation, maintaining field equipment and wireless data networks, and coordinating field schedules and staff. Candidates need an Associate's degree plus 4 years or a Bachelor's degree plus 2 years of relevant experience, with preference for plant identification skills, ecological dataset experience, and familiarity with field data-acquisition systems.
