Virginia Tech's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering is hiring a tenure-track Assistant Professor focused on the resilience, reliability, and cost of water and energy infrastructure, with emphasis on small and rural systems. The role involves developing an externally funded research program, teaching undergraduate and graduate courses, and mentoring students. A doctoral degree in civil engineering, environmental engineering, or a closely related field is required.
