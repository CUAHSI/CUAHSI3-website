The San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board is hiring an Environmental Scientist to implement field monitoring, analyze water quality data, and develop science-based strategies to meet water quality objectives in the SF Bay Region. The position is full-time and based in Oakland, CA, with periodic travel to field sites. Candidates must possess a valid driver's license and reside in California upon appointment.
