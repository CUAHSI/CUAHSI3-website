SRBC is hiring a full-time contracted term employee (three-year term) to coordinate the interstate Conowingo Watershed Implementation Plan across Pennsylvania, Maryland, and New York. The Coordinator will manage day-to-day administration, milestone tracking, and partner communication while also identifying new strategies, funding opportunities, and projects to accelerate nutrient reductions in support of Chesapeake Bay restoration. Candidates should be experienced environmental professionals with strong collaboration and project coordination skills; salary range is $74,000–$94,000.
