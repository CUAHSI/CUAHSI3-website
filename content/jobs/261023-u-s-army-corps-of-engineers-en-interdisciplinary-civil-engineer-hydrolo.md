The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers Seattle District is hiring a Senior Water Manager to oversee water management operations at Libby Dam, Albeni Falls Dam, and Chief Joseph Dam. The role involves directing reservoir operations, leading technical studies, coordinating with federal, international, and Tribal partners, and mentoring a team of engineers and scientists. Applicants must hold a Professional Engineering License or Hydrologist Certification and have at least one year of specialized experience in hydrologic engineering of multi-purpose dams and reservoirs.
