The Minnesota Department of Health's Environmental Health Division is hiring a Strategic Water Initiatives Director to lead strategic planning, policy development, and coordination within the Water Policy Center. The role involves assessing statewide water issues, engaging stakeholders, and advancing equitable access to safe drinking water. Applicants need four years of experience in strategic environmental planning and policy development, with knowledge of environmental health and water policy.
