The New Mexico Interstate Stream Commission is hiring a Lead Water Resources Specialist to manage the Strategic Water Reserve program, including water rights acquisitions, portfolio management, grant writing, and compliance with interstate water compacts and Endangered Species Act requirements. The role requires a bachelor's degree in a relevant science or engineering field plus six years of experience, or ten years of combined education and experience. Ideal candidates have backgrounds in water rights, hydrology, project management, and technical writing.
