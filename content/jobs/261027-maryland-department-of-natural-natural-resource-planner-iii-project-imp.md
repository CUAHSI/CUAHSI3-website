The Maryland DNR Watershed and Climate Services is hiring a long-term contractual Project Implementation Officer to manage multi-year restoration programs funded by the Chesapeake and Atlantic Coastal Bays Trust Fund, EPA grants, and federal infrastructure funding. The role involves grants management, project coordination, and technical support for Chesapeake Bay restoration initiatives. A bachelor's degree in environmental science or natural resources plus three years of relevant experience is required.
