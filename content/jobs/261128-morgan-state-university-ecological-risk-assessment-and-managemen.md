Morgan State University's Center for Urban and Coastal Climate Science Research is hiring a full-time faculty member at the assistant, associate, or full professor level to begin August 2027. The role involves establishing an independent funded research program, teaching undergraduate and graduate courses, and mentoring students in ecological risk assessment and related environmental sciences. Candidates must hold an earned Ph.D. in ecological risk assessment, environmental toxicology, or a closely related field with evidence of peer-reviewed scholarship.
