The Montana Department of Environmental Quality is hiring a Section Supervisor to lead the Nonpoint Source and Wetlands Section within the Water Quality Planning Bureau. The role involves supervising staff, directing section operations, managing program funding, and providing statewide leadership for nonpoint source pollution management and wetland conservation. Candidates need a bachelor's degree in an environmental or natural resource field and at least three years of progressively responsible experience including demonstrated leadership or supervision.
