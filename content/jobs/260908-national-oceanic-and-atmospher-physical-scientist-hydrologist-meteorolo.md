NOAA's National Weather Service is hiring Emergency Response Specialists at the GS-13 level across 10 locations to provide Impact-based Decision Support Services to emergency managers and decision makers during high-impact weather, water, and environmental events. Candidates must meet basic education requirements in physical science, hydrology, or meteorology and have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-12, including weather or environmental data analysis in support of emergency response and coordination with partners during high-impact events. Applicants must be U.S. citizens and able to obtain a Moderate Risk security clearance.
