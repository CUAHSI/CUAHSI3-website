The Utah Division of Water Rights is hiring an Engineer II or III for its Sevier River Office in Richfield. The role involves processing water rights applications, conducting field investigations, assisting with dam inspections, and supporting distribution systems. Candidates must hold an ABET-accredited engineering degree and have passed the FE exam (Engineer II) or hold a Utah PE license (Engineer III), with preference for experience in hydrology, hydraulics, and Utah water rights law.
