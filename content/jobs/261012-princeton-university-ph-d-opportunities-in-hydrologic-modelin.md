Princeton University's Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering and High Meadows Environmental Institute is seeking Ph.D. students for Fall 2027 to work on hydrologic modeling and scientific machine learning, centered on the Tiger-HLM modeling framework. Research spans large-scale hydrologic process representation, Arctic and cold-region hydrology, human-water interactions, and machine learning methods informed by physical principles. Candidates should have a degree in engineering, earth science, applied mathematics, computer science, or a related field with strong quantitative and computational skills.
