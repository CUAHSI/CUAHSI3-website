The Montana Department of Natural Resources & Conservation is hiring a Division Administrator to lead its Water Resources Division, a team of approximately 150 staff with a biennial budget exceeding $60 million. The role oversees statewide water rights administration, dam safety, floodplain management, and related programs. Candidates must have a bachelor's degree in a relevant field and at least five years of progressively responsible experience in regulatory or program administration, with senior-level leadership and budget management experience.
