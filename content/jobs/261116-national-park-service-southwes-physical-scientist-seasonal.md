The National Park Service is hiring a seasonal Physical Scientist at the GS-9 level to collect and analyze geologic, hydrologic, and geospatial field data in support of national park resource monitoring. The position runs approximately April through October 2027. Applicants must hold a relevant physical science degree with 24 semester hours in physical science and either one year of specialized GIS/field data experience at the GS-7 level or two years of directly related graduate education.
