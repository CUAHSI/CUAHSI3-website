The Arizona Department of Water Resources is hiring a Water Resource Specialist 2 or 3 to lead the Municipal Conservation Program within the Statewide AMA Section. The role involves coordinating review, data entry, verification, and analysis of Annual Water Withdrawal and Use Reports submitted by regulated municipal users. Candidates must have at least 2-7 years of professional water resource management experience depending on level, or equivalent combinations of education and experience.
