The Bureau of Land Management is hiring seasonal Physical Science Technicians at the GS-05, GS-06, and GS-07 grade levels for field and laboratory work supporting air, water, soil, and environmental monitoring programs. Duties include data collection, sample processing, equipment operation, and report preparation. Candidates must have relevant specialized experience or education in a physical science field such as hydrology, chemistry, or physics.
