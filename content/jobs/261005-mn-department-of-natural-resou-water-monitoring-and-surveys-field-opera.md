The Minnesota Department of Natural Resources is hiring a Hydrologist Supervisor to direct statewide water quantity data collection and oversee stream gaging, observation well, wetland, and climate monitoring networks. The role involves supervising field staff, providing expert hydrologic and hydraulic analyses, and supporting water resource management decisions. Candidates must have a relevant bachelor's degree with qualifying hydrology coursework and at least four years of advanced professional experience including leadership or supervisory responsibilities.
