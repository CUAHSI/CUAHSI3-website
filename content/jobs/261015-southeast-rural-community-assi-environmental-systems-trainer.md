SERCAP, a regional nonprofit providing technical assistance to low-income rural communities, is hiring an Environmental Systems Trainer to develop and deliver curriculum on drinking water and wastewater systems. The role involves instructional design for in-person and online training, subject matter expertise on small water and wastewater systems, and coordination with federal partners and regional staff. Candidates should have at least five years of combined experience in training and water/wastewater fields, with operator certification highly desirable.
