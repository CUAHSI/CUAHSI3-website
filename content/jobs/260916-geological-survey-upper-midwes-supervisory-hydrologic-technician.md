USGS Upper Midwest Water Science Center is hiring a GS-12 Supervisory Hydrologic Technician to lead staff in surface water, groundwater, and water-quality data collection and analysis. The role involves supervising field operations, managing real-time hydrologic data quality, and acting as liaison to hydrologic organizations. Applicants must have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-11, including planning and conducting data computation for approximately 30 or more gaging stations and acting as party chief for site installations and flood flow measurements.
