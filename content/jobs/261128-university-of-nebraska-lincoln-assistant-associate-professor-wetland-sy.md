The School of Natural Resources at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln is hiring a tenure-track assistant or associate professor in wetland systems science. The position is split 50% research and 50% teaching, with research focused on wetlands and aquatic-terrestrial interfaces in agricultural working landscapes. Applicants must hold a Ph.D. in a relevant field and demonstrate expertise in wetland ecology, hydrology, biogeochemistry, or related disciplines.
