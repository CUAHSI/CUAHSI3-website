The Utah Department of Environmental Quality is hiring a Division Director to lead its Division of Drinking Water, overseeing a team of 55 staff, managing state drinking water programs and funding, and shaping related policy. The role reports directly to the DEQ Commissioner and involves collaboration with elected officials, federal agencies, and stakeholders statewide. Candidates must hold a qualifying four-year degree and have executive experience in an environmental program, preferably related to drinking water, along with budget management and legislative experience.
