The Arizona Department of Water Resources is hiring a senior manager to oversee the Assured and Adequate Water Supply Programs, which evaluate 100-year water supply availability before homes can be sold. The role involves supervising a team of hydrogeologists and water resource specialists, developing policy, and coordinating with agency leadership and external stakeholders. Candidates must have strong managerial experience and expertise in water resources planning, policy analysis, and regulatory interpretation.
