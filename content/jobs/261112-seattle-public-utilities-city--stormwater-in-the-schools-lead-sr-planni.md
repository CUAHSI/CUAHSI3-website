Seattle Public Utilities is hiring a full-time Stormwater in the Schools Lead to manage science-based water quality and salmon curriculum programs for K-12 schools, primarily at the elementary level. The role involves building partnerships with schools and tribes, managing contracts and budgets, and meeting stormwater regulatory engagement requirements. Candidates must have a bachelor's degree in a related field plus 3 years of relevant planning/environmental experience, or 5 years of equivalent experience without a degree.
