NRRI at the University of Minnesota is hiring a temporary Associate Staff Scientist to conduct field and laboratory work assessing aquatic ecosystem health in Great Lakes coastal wetlands, streams, and inland lakes. The role involves fish and invertebrate sampling, water quality monitoring, laboratory sample processing, and data management. A bachelor's degree in a relevant natural resource discipline plus two years of experience (or six years of equivalent experience), along with boat handling skills and field identification of Great Lakes fish species, is required.
