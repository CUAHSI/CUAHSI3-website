Cornell University's Critical Infrastructure Systems Lab is hiring a Postdoctoral Associate to develop machine learning and remote sensing workflows for global-scale mapping of critical water infrastructure as part of the DARE project. The role involves building scalable computational pipelines and integrating datasets into large-scale hydrologic models. Candidates must hold a PhD in Computer Science, Civil/Environmental Engineering, Remote Sensing, Geospatial Science, or a related field, with strong expertise in deep learning for geospatial data and high-performance GPU computing.
