Cadmus is hiring a Data Analyst to support EPA and other government agencies on water quality projects related to the Clean Water Act and Safe Drinking Water Act. The role involves data cleaning, analysis, and visualization using R, Python, and ArcGIS, as well as technical writing and stakeholder engagement. Candidates need 2–4 years of relevant professional experience and a bachelor's degree in an environmental or related field.
