TCEQ's Water Availability Division is hiring two full-time Hydrologists (levels III and IV) in Austin to review Texas surface water right applications using Water Availability Models, GIS, and hydrologic principles. Candidates must hold a relevant bachelor's degree plus 4–6 years of hydrologic experience (or advanced degree with reduced experience). Key qualifications include surface water rights experience, familiarity with WAMs/WRAP, streamflow data, databases, Excel, and ArcGIS Pro.
