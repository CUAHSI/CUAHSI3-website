The Washington State Department of Ecology's Water Quality Program is hiring two Environmental Specialist 4s to serve as Watershed Implementation Leads for the Hangman and Palouse watersheds in eastern Washington. The roles involve developing and implementing water quality clean-up plans (TMDLs and STIs), coordinating riparian restoration with landowners and partners, and using regulatory tools to achieve compliance. Candidates need nine years of combined experience and/or education in environmental or natural sciences with demonstrated skills in regulatory compliance, plan development, and partnership building.
