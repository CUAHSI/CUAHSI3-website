The Connecticut DEEP is hiring a full-time Environmental Engineer 3 to work in its Underground Injection Control Program, protecting groundwater through permitting, engineering review, and regulatory determinations related to subsurface wastewater disposal and water reuse. The role requires an experienced engineer with strong problem-solving, organizational, and communication skills; direct wastewater or groundwater experience is preferred but related environmental engineering backgrounds are accepted. The position is hybrid, based in Hartford, CT, and pays $84,447–$115,832 per year.
