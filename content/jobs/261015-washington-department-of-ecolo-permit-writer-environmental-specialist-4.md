The Washington Department of Ecology's Water Resources Program is hiring a Permit Writer to manage, evaluate, and draft water right decisions under Washington Water Law. The role involves hydrologic/hydrogeologic data analysis, field investigations, GIS mapping, and regulatory compliance reviews. Candidates need at least five years of relevant experience combined with a bachelor's degree in an environmental or natural science field, or equivalent combinations of education and experience.
