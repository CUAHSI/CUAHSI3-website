Cadmus is hiring an entry-level Water Policy Junior Analyst to support projects for the U.S. EPA and other government clients on topics such as lead in drinking water, emerging contaminants, and water system financing. The role involves research, data analysis, writing policy documents, and developing training and outreach materials. A BS/BA in public health, environmental science, or a related field is required, and candidates must be eligible to work in the U.S.
