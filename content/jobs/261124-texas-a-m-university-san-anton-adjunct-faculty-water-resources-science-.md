Texas A&M University–San Antonio's Department of Natural Sciences is hiring a part-time, non-tenure-track adjunct faculty member to teach the Water Laws, Rules and Policy course in the Water Resources Science and Technology Program. The position requires a Master of Science in Environmental Science, Policy, and Management or a related field with at least 18 graduate credit hours in the discipline. A completed Ph.D. and college-level teaching experience are preferred.
