The Oregon Water Resources Department is hiring a full-time GIS Analyst for its Information Technology team. The role involves building custom maps, performing data analysis, and providing GIS support using ArcGIS Pro for internal and external customers. Candidates must have three years of information systems experience or an associate's degree in a related field plus one year of experience, with preference given to those with ArcGIS Pro skills.
