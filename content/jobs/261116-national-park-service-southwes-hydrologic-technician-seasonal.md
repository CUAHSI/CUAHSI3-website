The National Park Service's Southwest Alaska Inventory and Monitoring Network is hiring 1-2 seasonal hydrologic technicians at GS-06 through GS-08 grade levels for May–October 2027. The positions involve collecting water quality and quantity field data, calibrating and maintaining hydrologic monitoring equipment, and performing data management and quality control across five Alaskan national parks. Candidates must have specialized experience or graduate-level education in hydrology or a related water resources field.
