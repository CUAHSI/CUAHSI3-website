AWWA is hiring a Technical Programs Lead within its Education and Engineering Services group to develop technical programs, products, and content for water professionals. The role involves coordinating technical reviews, leading conference programming, managing volunteer committees, and producing research and educational resources. Candidates need a bachelor's degree in an engineering or water-related field and at least five years of experience in the water sector, with strong technical writing and project management skills.
