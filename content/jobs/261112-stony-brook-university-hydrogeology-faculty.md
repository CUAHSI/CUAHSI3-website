The Department of Geosciences at Stony Brook University invites applications for a full-time tenure-track or tenured faculty position at the Assistant or Associate Professor level in hydrogeology, broadly defined. The role includes undergraduate teaching, graduate courses for the department's Masters program in Geology (Hydrogeology track), and liaison with local environmental and geotechnical consulting firms. The posting opened October 27, 2026; the deadline is January 15, 2027, 11:59 pm Eastern.
