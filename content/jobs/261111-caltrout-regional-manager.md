CalTrout is hiring a full-time Regional Manager for its North Coast program, based in Arcata, CA, to lead conservation and restoration efforts in northern Humboldt and Del Norte counties. The role involves strategic planning, staff supervision, grant and project management, and partnership development with Tribal Nations and resource agencies. A bachelor's degree in a related field and proven experience managing environmental restoration programs are required, with preference for familiarity with the Klamath Basin and surrounding watersheds.
