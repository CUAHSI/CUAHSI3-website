The National Park Service is hiring a GS-12 Quantitative Landscape Ecologist for Everglades National Park's Resilience and Sustainability Team. The role involves operating ecosystem models, analyzing restoration scenarios, and supporting large-scale interagency Everglades restoration initiatives. Applicants must hold a qualifying biology-related degree and have at least one year of specialized experience using landscape-scale ecological models and spatial data analysis tools such as R or Python.
