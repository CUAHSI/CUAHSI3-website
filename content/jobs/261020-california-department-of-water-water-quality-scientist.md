The California Department of Water Resources is hiring a permanent-intermittent Environmental Scientist to support its Continuous Environmental Monitoring unit in the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta. The role involves operating water quality monitoring equipment, collecting and processing environmental samples, managing data sets, and contributing to technical reports related to San Francisco Estuary management. Candidates must hold a valid driver's license, be able to work fieldwork hours including nights and weekends, and meet physical requirements for boat-based fieldwork.
