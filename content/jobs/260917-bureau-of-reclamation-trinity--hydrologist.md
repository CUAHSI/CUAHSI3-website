The Bureau of Reclamation is hiring a Hydrologist (GS-09/11/12) to serve as the lead physical scientist for hydrology and geomorphology on the Trinity River Restoration Program Science Branch team. Responsibilities include hydrologic and hydraulic modeling, field investigations, technical reporting, and providing scientific input for river restoration projects. Applicants must hold a degree in physical or natural science or engineering with at least 30 semester hours in relevant coursework including calculus and physics, plus qualifying specialized experience or graduate education.
