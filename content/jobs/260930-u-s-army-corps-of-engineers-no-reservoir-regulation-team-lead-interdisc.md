The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers is hiring a team lead for the Regulation Unit in the Reservoir Control Center to manage day-to-day reservoir regulation across the Columbia River Basin for hydropower, flood risk, endangered species recovery, navigation, and water supply. The role involves coordinating with federal agencies, utilities, and other stakeholders on reservoir operations and scheduling. Applicants must be U.S. citizens with a qualifying degree in hydrology, civil engineering, or a related field and one year of specialized experience leading or executing multi-purpose water resources and reservoir regulation operations at the GS-12 equivalent level.
