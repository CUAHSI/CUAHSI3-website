The National Audubon Society is hiring a Senior Policy Manager to advance western water policy and government affairs in support of bird conservation. The role involves developing and implementing federal, regional, and state water policy strategies, leading advocacy in Congress and with agencies, and coordinating with NGO coalitions including on Colorado River Basin issues. Candidates need a bachelor's degree and 7+ years of experience in legal, policy, or advocacy work, with preference for experience in water policy and wildlife issues.
