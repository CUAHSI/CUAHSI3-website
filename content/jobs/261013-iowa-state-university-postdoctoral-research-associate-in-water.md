The Department of Agricultural and Biosystems Engineering at Iowa State University is hiring a Postdoctoral Research Associate to conduct interdisciplinary research on watershed hydrology, agricultural water quality, and antimicrobial resistance in agricultural landscapes. The role involves developing and applying watershed models (e.g., SWAT+), deploying environmental monitoring systems, and analyzing complex datasets. Candidates must hold a Ph.D. in a relevant engineering or environmental science field and have demonstrated experience in watershed modeling, AMR research, and scientific programming.
