The Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board is hiring a Water Resource Control Engineer to implement its Land Disposal Program, regulating landfills, composting facilities, and surface impoundments that may impact surface or groundwater. The role requires engineering knowledge and technical expertise to protect water quality, particularly drinking water sources. All experience levels will be considered.
