The National Park Service is hiring a Physical Scientist at Redwood National Park to develop and implement programs related to geology, soils, hydrology, air quality, and related physical sciences. Duties include writing management plans, designing monitoring protocols, conducting data analysis, and coordinating with other agencies on natural resource protection. Candidates must have a degree in physical science or related field and specialized experience in physical or abiotic resource management, qualifying at the GS-09 or GS-11 level.
