The University of Michigan SEAS and Dr. Tiantian Yang's lab are hiring a postdoctoral fellow to conduct research in hydrologic modeling, flood forecasting, snow modeling, soil moisture analytics, and AI/ML applications for water resources decision-making. The role also involves mentoring graduate students, contributing to grant proposals, and publishing research. A completed PhD in hydrology, water resources engineering, remote sensing, data science, or a related field is required.
