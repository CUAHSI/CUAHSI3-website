The National Park Service is hiring a Physical Scientist at Redwood National Park to develop and implement programs in geology, soils, hydrology, air quality, and related physical sciences supporting natural resource stewardship and restoration. The role involves writing management plans, designing monitoring protocols, conducting data analysis, and coordinating with agencies and the public. Applicants must meet GS-1301 basic education requirements and have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-09 level in abiotic resource protection and management.
