American Rivers is hiring a Director of River Restoration for the Mid-Atlantic Region to lead a portfolio of dam removal, floodplain restoration, and related river conservation projects from concept through construction and closeout. The role involves managing project budgets, contracts, consultant teams, permitting, and grant compliance while coordinating with landowners, agency partners, and funders. Candidates must have seven or more years of progressively responsible project management experience in river restoration, engineering, construction, or a closely related field.
