The New Jersey DEP Division of Water Quality is hiring a permanent Research Scientist 1 to conduct and review water quality analyses, assist permit writers with waste load allocations and effluent limits, and supervise technical staff. The position requires a Master's degree in a relevant field and four years of related experience. Eligibility is limited to permanent DEP employees working in the Division of Water Quality.
