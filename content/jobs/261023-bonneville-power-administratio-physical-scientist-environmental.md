Bonneville Power Administration is hiring a Physical Scientist (Environmental) at the GS-11 or GS-12 level to support pollution prevention and abatement programs across BPA facilities in the Pacific Northwest. The role involves environmental field sampling, site remediation oversight, emergency spill response, and ensuring compliance with federal and state environmental regulations. Candidates must have a degree with 24 semester hours in physical science or related engineering science and at least one year of specialized experience in environmental sampling and regulatory compliance (GS-11) or equivalent at the GS-12 level.
