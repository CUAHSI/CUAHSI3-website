The New York State Department of Environmental Conservation is hiring a Research Scientist 1 to support water quality monitoring and applied research within the Bureau of Water Assessment and Management. The role involves data collection and analysis for streams, lakes, and groundwater across NYS, with a focus on total dissolved solids, benthic macroinvertebrates, and emerging contaminants. Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree in a relevant environmental or biological field plus two years of professional research experience.
