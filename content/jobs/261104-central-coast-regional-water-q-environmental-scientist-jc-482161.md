The Central Coast Regional Water Quality Control Board is hiring an Environmental Scientist for its Irrigated Lands Program. The role involves scientific, regulatory compliance, enforcement, and field investigation work related to discharges from irrigated agricultural land. Candidates must meet California state civil service requirements and apply through CalCareers.
