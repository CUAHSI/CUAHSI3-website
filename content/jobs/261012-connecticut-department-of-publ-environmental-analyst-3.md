The Connecticut Department of Public Health is hiring an Environmental Analyst 3 to lead the Lead and Copper Rule Implementation Unit within its Drinking Water Section. The role involves overseeing statewide compliance with lead and copper drinking water regulations, guiding lower-level analysts, and providing technical and regulatory assistance to water systems and operators. Candidates must meet minimum qualifications in environmental analysis as specified in the job class specification.
