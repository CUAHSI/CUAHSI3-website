UC Santa Barbara's Earth Research Institute is hiring a Hub Coordinator (classified as Project Policy Analyst 3) to manage operations, partnerships, communications, and events for the new Groundwater Research Hub. The role requires a bachelor's degree in a relevant field plus 4–6 years of experience coordinating research programs or multi-stakeholder projects, including budget responsibility and external partner engagement. The position also involves supervising data scientists and student assistants and translating groundwater science into policy-relevant communications.
