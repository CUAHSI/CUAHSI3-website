The Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma is hiring a Water Resource Planner to develop watershed planning documents, drought management strategies, and technical analyses supporting sustainable water resource management and implementation of the tribe's Water Settlement Agreement. The role involves collaborating with state, federal, and local partners, conducting data analysis, and providing community outreach. A Bachelor of Science degree with 2 years of relevant experience, or 6 years of experience in lieu of a degree, is required along with knowledge of water law, GIS, and technical report development.
