The California Department of Water Resources is hiring multiple Engineers, Water Resources for its River Forecasting Unit to maintain and calibrate hydrologic models and prepare river and reservoir inflow forecasts. Candidates must meet the minimum qualifications in the Engineer, Water Resources classification specification, including relevant engineering education and experience. The positions are permanent, full-time, hybrid roles based in Sacramento County.
