Stony Brook University's School of Marine and Atmospheric Sciences is hiring a tenure-track assistant professor in Marine Sciences with a focus on hydrodynamic modeling, including coastal/estuarine circulation, coupled physical-biogeochemical, or related modeling approaches. The position requires a Ph.D. in oceanography, marine science, or a related field by August 2027. The successful candidate is expected to teach at the undergraduate and graduate levels and establish an externally funded research program.
