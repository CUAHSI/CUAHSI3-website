The Vermont Department of Environmental Conservation Stormwater Program is hiring a full-time Environmental Analyst to lead regulatory, technical, and compliance activities for construction and industrial stormwater permits across an assigned regional district. The role involves reviewing engineering plans and stormwater pollution prevention plans, conducting field inspections, providing technical guidance, and supporting enforcement actions under permits including the CGP, INDC, and MSGP. Candidates must have a relevant bachelor's or master's degree with at least one year of experience in a natural resources field, or four or more years of experience in an engineering, environmental, or natural resources field.
