EPA Region 8 is hiring a GS-13 scientist or engineer to support the Underground Injection Control permit program within the Safe Drinking Water Branch. The role involves providing technical advice, overseeing state and Tribal UIC permitting programs, and resolving complex technical and regulatory issues. Applicants must have one year of specialized experience at the GS-12 level involving review of permit applications, geological suitability assessments, and well log analysis.
