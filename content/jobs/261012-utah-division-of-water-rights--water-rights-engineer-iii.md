The Utah Division of Water Rights is hiring a Water Rights Engineer (I, II, or III level depending on qualifications) to assist the public with compliance and enforcement of state water rights statutes and rules. Responsibilities include investigating enforcement referrals, issuing orders, participating in hearings, and supporting regional offices. Minimum qualification is a completed engineering degree; PE licensure is required for the Engineer III level.
