Stanford University's Department of Civil & Environmental Engineering is hiring a tenure-track Assistant Professor focused on water or air quality, including areas such as physicochemical treatment, resource recovery, and air quality engineering. Applicants must hold or be completing a PhD in a relevant field and demonstrate research potential and commitment to teaching. The role involves teaching at graduate and undergraduate levels and leading a PhD research group.
