The Oregon Water Resources Department is hiring a Certificates Program Analyst to review Claims of Beneficial Use and determine compliance with water right permit conditions. The role involves research, legal/policy interpretation, and serving as lead contact with the Oregon State Board of Examiners for Engineering and Land Surveying. Candidates should have experience in natural resources, regulatory frameworks, and public communication.
