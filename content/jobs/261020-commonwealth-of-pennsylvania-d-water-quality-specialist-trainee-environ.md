The Pennsylvania Department of Environmental Protection is hiring a Water Quality Specialist Trainee in Scranton to conduct inspections of oil and gas activities, investigate water quality complaints, and collect environmental samples. The position is a civil service permanent full-time role focused on building foundational skills in environmental compliance and enforcement. Applicants must have a bachelor's degree in environmental science, geology, biology, or a related field, or four years of equivalent technical experience.
