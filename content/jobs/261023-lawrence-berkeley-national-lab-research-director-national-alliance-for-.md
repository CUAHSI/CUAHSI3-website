Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory is hiring a Research Director to lead the research program for NAWI, a DOE Energy Innovation Hub focused on water treatment technologies and regional water planning. The role involves overseeing a multi-institutional research portfolio, managing Topic Area Leads, and collaborating with DOE and partners across national labs, academia, industry, and utilities. A minimum of 10 years of experience in water treatment technology development, regional water planning, or closely related fields is required.
