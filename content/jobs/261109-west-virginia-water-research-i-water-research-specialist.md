The West Virginia Water Research Institute at WVU is hiring a Water Research Specialist to support water resources research programs. The role involves field water quality sampling, data management and analysis, and outreach activities across state, federal, and privately funded grants. Candidates should have experience with field sampling protocols, water quality monitoring equipment, GIS, and stakeholder engagement.
