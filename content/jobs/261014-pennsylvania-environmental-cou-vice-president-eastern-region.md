The Pennsylvania Environmental Council is hiring a Vice President to lead Eastern Region programs and statewide water initiatives, reporting to the President and serving on the senior leadership team. The role oversees Northeast and Southeast regional programs including watershed protection, conservation, trails, and outdoor recreation, while managing staff and fundraising. Candidates need 8–10 years of progressive leadership experience in environmental, conservation, or nonprofit management, with strong knowledge of Pennsylvania water policy and proven fundraising skills.
