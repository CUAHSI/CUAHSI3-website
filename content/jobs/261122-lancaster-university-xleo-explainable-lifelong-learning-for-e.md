Lancaster University is recruiting a PhD student under supervisors Prof Plamen Angelov, Prof Peter M. Atkinson, and Prof Ana Basiri to develop prototype-based continual learning methods for Earth observation tasks including flood monitoring and multimodal environmental monitoring. The project addresses catastrophic forgetting in deep learning models applied to continuous Sentinel satellite data streams. Candidates should have a background in machine learning, remote sensing, or a closely related field.
