UC Berkeley's Coastal Climate Resilience Lab is hiring a part-time (20 hrs/week) Research Data Analyst 1 to support remote sensing and geospatial analysis projects focused on freshwater plumes and nearshore marine environments. The role involves applying existing code and established methods to ecological datasets, performing statistical analysis, and creating data visualizations under direct supervision. A bachelor's degree in a related field and basic skills in statistical analysis and geospatial tools are required.
