The Nature Conservancy is hiring a Project Manager to lead riparian and wet meadow restoration projects across southern Utah in partnership with the Bureau of Land Management and local conservation organizations. The role involves watershed assessment, project design, permitting, field implementation, and partner coordination. Candidates should have a bachelor's degree and at least five years of experience in natural resource management, including designing and implementing restoration projects.
