UMass Boston's School for the Environment is hiring a full-time, non-tenure-track Visiting Assistant Professor in Coastal Engineering to begin January 1, 2027, for one year with possible renewal. The position involves teaching one course per semester, mentoring students, and contributing to research on coastal processes, nature-based infrastructure, and climate adaptation through the Stone Living Lab. Candidates must hold a PhD in Coastal Engineering, Civil Engineering, Ocean Engineering, or a related field by the start date.
