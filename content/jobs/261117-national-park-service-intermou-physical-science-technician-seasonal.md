The National Park Service Intermountain Region is hiring seasonal Physical Science Technicians at the GS-05/06/07 levels for work anticipated March–July 2027. Positions involve field and laboratory data collection, sample processing, equipment operation, and reporting in support of air, water, and environmental monitoring programs. Candidates must have relevant specialized experience or a physical science degree at the qualifying grade level.
