The USGS Pennsylvania Water Science Center is hiring a GS-12 Supervisory Hydrologic Technician to lead staff conducting hydrologic data collection, analysis, and reporting across Pennsylvania. The role involves supervising surface, ground, and water-quality data operations, managing cooperator relations, and advising on new data collection technologies. Applicants must have one year of specialized experience equivalent to the GS-11 level, including planning and computing data from approximately 30 or more gaging stations and conducting hydrologic studies.
