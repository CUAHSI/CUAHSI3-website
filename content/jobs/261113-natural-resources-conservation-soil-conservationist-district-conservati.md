USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service is hiring a District Conservationist for the Marianna, Arkansas field office to manage agricultural resources, develop soil and water conservation plans, and provide technical guidance to landowners and partner agencies. The position is filled at the GS-11 or GS-12 grade level. Candidates must have a degree in soil conservation or a related agricultural or natural resource discipline and qualifying specialized experience or graduate education.
