The Vermont Department of Environmental Conservation is hiring an Environmental Analyst for its Rutland North Regional Office to review and permit wastewater systems and potable water supplies. The role involves site visits, soil review, engineering plan review, permit issuance, and compliance monitoring. Candidates must hold a relevant degree plus two or more years of experience in a natural resources field, or six or more years of relevant experience.
