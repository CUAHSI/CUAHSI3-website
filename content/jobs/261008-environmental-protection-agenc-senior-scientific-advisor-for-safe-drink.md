The EPA Office of Water is hiring a Senior Scientific Advisor to provide scientific and research guidance on Safe Drinking Water Act programs to senior leadership. The role involves directing drinking water research and development, advising on health effects, risk assessment, treatment technologies, and analytical methods, and coordinating with interagency partners on water quality and security issues. Candidates must be U.S. citizens with at least 3 years of specialized experience in national drinking water quality assessment and at least 1 year of experience equivalent to the GS-15 level.
