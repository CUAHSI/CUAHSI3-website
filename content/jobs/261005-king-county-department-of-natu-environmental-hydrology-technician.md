King County's Water and Land Resources Division is hiring an Environmental Hydrology Technician to monitor groundwater levels, support stream gauge networks, collect water quality samples, and analyze and communicate environmental data. The role involves approximately 4-5 days of field work per month across King County. A bachelor's degree in environmental studies, geology, or hydrology, or two years of relevant professional experience is required.
