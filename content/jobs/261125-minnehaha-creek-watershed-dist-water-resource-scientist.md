Minnehaha Creek Watershed District is hiring a Water Resource Scientist for its Research and Monitoring Department to collect field data, conduct water quality and hydrologic analysis, and develop watershed models. The role involves data analysis, modeling, and data visualization to support watershed restoration strategies and MCWD projects. Candidates should have a bachelor's degree in hydrology, environmental science, or a related field plus 2-5 years of relevant experience, including proficiency in R or Python.
