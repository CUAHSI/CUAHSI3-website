The Bureau of Reclamation is hiring a Natural Resource Specialist at the GS-09 level under the Direct Hire Authority for College Graduates. The role involves conducting environmental analyses, ensuring compliance with laws such as NEPA, ESA, and Clean Water Act, and leading field data collection for aquatic and habitat surveys. Applicants must have a degree in biological sciences or a related field and either one year of specialized experience at the GS-07 level or two years of relevant graduate education.
