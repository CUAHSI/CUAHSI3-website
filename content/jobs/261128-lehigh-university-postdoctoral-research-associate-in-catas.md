Lehigh University's Center for Catastrophe Modeling and Resilience is hiring a postdoctoral research associate to work on flood hazard modeling, flood risk and loss estimation, and AI integration into catastrophe modeling. The position is supervised by associate director Ethan Yang and associate professor Hong-Yi Li within a multi-university consortium. Applicants must hold a doctoral degree in Civil and Environmental Engineering, Water Resources Engineering, Hydrology, or a closely related field by the start date.
