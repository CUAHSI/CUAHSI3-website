UW-Madison Division of Extension is hiring a Natural Resources Water Educator based in Wood County (Wisconsin Rapids, WI) to design, deliver, and evaluate research-based educational programming on water quality, conservation, and watershed management. The role involves building partnerships among farmers, residents, watershed organizations, and local officials to advance community-driven water resource solutions. A bachelor's degree in a relevant natural or environmental science field and at least 2 years of related work experience are required.
