UC San Diego's CW3E is hiring a Research and Operations Hydrologist to be embedded at the NOAA Southeast River Forecast Center in Peachtree City, GA, funded by a CIROH grant. The role involves bridging research and operational hydrology, including developing ensemble hydrologic forecasts, supporting Forecast Informed Reservoir Operations (FIRO), and helping implement new modeling tools. A bachelor's degree in hydrology or civil engineering is required, with a master's preferred, along with experience in large-scale hydrologic modeling and High-Performance Computing.
