Coachella Valley Water District is hiring an Environmental Services Administrator to direct the Water Quality and Compliance Division within its Environmental Services Department. The role involves managing programs, staff, budgets, and regulatory compliance related to water quality, stormwater, groundwater, and wastewater source control. Candidates need a relevant bachelor's degree, eight years of progressively responsible experience in water quality or related fields, and at least five years of supervisory experience.
