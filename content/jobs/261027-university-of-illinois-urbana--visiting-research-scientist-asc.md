The University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign's Agroecosystem Sustainability Center is hiring a Visiting Research Scientist to lead research on agricultural hydrology, water quality, and sustainable agroecosystems. The role involves publishing research, supporting grant proposal development, and contributing to project management and outreach. A PhD in a relevant field and at least two years of post-PhD experience in agricultural hydrology or related areas are required.
