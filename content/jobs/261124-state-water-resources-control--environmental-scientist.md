The California State Water Resources Control Board's Central Valley Region is hiring an Environmental Scientist for the Mercury and Metals TMDL Unit in the Planning Section. The role involves performing scientific research, analysis, and regulatory work to assess water quality impairments and develop pollutant load reduction targets under the Clean Water Act. Candidates must meet California civil service eligibility requirements and possess relevant environmental science qualifications.
