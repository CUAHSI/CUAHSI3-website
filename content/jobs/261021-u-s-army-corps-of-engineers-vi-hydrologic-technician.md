The U.S. Army Corps of Engineers is hiring a Hydrologic Technician at the GS-5 through GS-11 level in Vicksburg, Mississippi. The position involves collecting and maintaining real-time meteorological, hydrologic, hydraulic, and limnologic data using the Corps Water Management System, operating field equipment, and potentially serving as a team leader. Applicants must meet specialized experience requirements appropriate to the grade level applied for, with education substitution permitted only at GS-5.
