The Vermont Department of Environmental Conservation Stormwater Program is hiring a full-time Environmental Analyst to lead regulatory, technical, and compliance activities for construction and industrial stormwater general and individual permits. The role involves reviewing engineering plans and stormwater pollution prevention plans, conducting field inspections, providing technical guidance on erosion prevention and sediment control, and supporting enforcement actions across an assigned regional district. Candidates must have a relevant degree plus experience, or at least five years of experience in an engineering, environmental, or natural resources field.
