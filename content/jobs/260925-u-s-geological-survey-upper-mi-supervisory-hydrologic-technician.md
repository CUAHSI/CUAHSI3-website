USGS Upper Midwest Water Science Center is hiring a GS-12 Supervisory Hydrologic Technician to lead the Grayling Field Office. The role involves supervising staff in surface water, groundwater, and water-quality data collection and analysis, overseeing real-time data management, and representing USGS at technical meetings. Candidates must have at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-11, including planning and conducting data collection from approximately 30 or more gaging stations and acting as party chief for field installations.
