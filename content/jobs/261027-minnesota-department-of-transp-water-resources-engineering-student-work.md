The Minnesota Department of Transportation Metro District is hiring up to two student workers for summer 2027 to assist with inventory and inspections of hydraulic water resources infrastructure, GIS/GPS analysis, and engineering automation support. Applicants must be currently enrolled in a college or university pursuing a degree in Civil Engineering, Soil Science, Hydraulics, Natural Resources, Environmental Science, GIS, or a related field. A valid Class D Driver's License is required.
