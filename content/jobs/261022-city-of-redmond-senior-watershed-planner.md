The City of Redmond Public Works department is hiring a Senior Watershed Planner to lead watershed and stormwater basin planning, NPDES permit compliance, and long-range utility planning. The role involves managing interdisciplinary projects, community engagement, and capital improvement coordination related to stormwater and surface water systems. A bachelor's degree in a related field and 4–6 years of professional experience in stormwater or watershed planning are required.
