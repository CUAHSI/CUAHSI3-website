The New Mexico Office of the State Engineer is hiring a water resources professional for its District VI Office in Santa Fe to administer water rights, process permit applications, perform surface and groundwater field measurements, and assist the public with water rights matters. The role requires a bachelor's degree in hydrology, engineering, environmental science, or a related field, or four years of equivalent experience. Key qualifications include experience with water rights administration, groundwater and surface water measurement, and geospatial data tools.
