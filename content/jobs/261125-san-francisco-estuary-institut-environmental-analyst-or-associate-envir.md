The San Francisco Estuary Institute is hiring an early-career environmental engineer or scientist for its Clean Water Program to support hydrodynamic and biogeochemical modeling of San Francisco Bay and the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta. The role involves running and improving coupled models to study nutrient cycling, dissolved oxygen, and harmful algal blooms in support of the Nutrient Management Strategy. A bachelor's degree plus 2 years of relevant experience, or a master's degree, in a field involving hydrodynamic or biogeochemical modeling is required.
