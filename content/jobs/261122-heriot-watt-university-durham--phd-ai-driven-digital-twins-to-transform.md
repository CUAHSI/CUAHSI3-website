Heriot-Watt University and Durham University are recruiting a PhD student to develop a neuro-algebraic digital twin for green flood management infrastructure, focusing on rain gardens and slope stability under extreme weather. The project combines machine learning, formal algebraic modelling, and physical experimentation using instrumented rain gardens and lysimeter facilities. Candidates should have a background relevant to hydrology, geotechnical engineering, or computational modelling with interest in AI-driven simulation.
