Auburn University's Department of Biosystems Engineering is hiring an Assistant Research Professor to develop a research program in emerging contaminants and environmental remediation using nature-based solutions and innovative treatment technologies. The position is a full-time, non-tenure track, annually renewable research appointment. A Ph.D. in biosystems, biological, agricultural engineering, or a closely related engineering discipline is required.
