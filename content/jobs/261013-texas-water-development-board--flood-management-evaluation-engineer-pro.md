The Texas Water Development Board is hiring an engineer or project manager for its Flood Management Evaluation Department to oversee hydrologic and hydraulic studies, manage engineering consultant contracts, and coordinate flood risk reduction efforts for smaller and rural Texas communities. Candidates must hold a Texas P.E. license, a P.E. license from another U.S. state, or an Engineer in Training (E.I.T.) certification. The role involves technical review of watershed study reports, contractor oversight, and liaison work with regional flood planning groups and local stakeholders.
