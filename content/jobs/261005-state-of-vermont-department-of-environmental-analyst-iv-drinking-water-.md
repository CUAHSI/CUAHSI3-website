The Vermont Department of Environmental Conservation is hiring a Compliance Analyst for its Drinking Water & Groundwater Protection Division to support regulatory and technical oversight of public drinking water systems, with a focus on Lead and Copper Rule compliance. This is a limited-service (non-tenured) full-time position funded through September 2027. Candidates must hold a relevant bachelor's degree plus one year of experience, a master's degree, or four or more years of experience in an environmental or related field.
