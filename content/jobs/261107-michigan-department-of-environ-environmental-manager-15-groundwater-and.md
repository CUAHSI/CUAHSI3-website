Michigan EGLE is hiring a section manager to oversee the Groundwater and Geological Services Section within the Geological Resources Management Division, including staff managing water withdrawal registration, groundwater monitoring, and data systems. The role involves supervising professional and technical staff, coordinating with state and national groundwater programs, and advising EGLE leadership on groundwater policy. Candidates must have a bachelor's degree in a relevant science or engineering field and seven years of professional experience, including supervisory or specialist experience at senior levels.
