DNREC's Division of Watershed Stewardship is hiring a Division Director to lead and manage the division's programs, staff, and annual operating budget of over $20M. The role involves developing and implementing divisional policies, representing the Department at meetings, and providing technical and administrative direction across three sections: Conservation Programs, Shoreline & Waterway Management, and Watershed Assessment & Management. Candidates must have experience in strategic planning, operations management, policy administration, and financial administration.
