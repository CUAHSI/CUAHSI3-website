Water Rights, Inc. is hiring a full-time Water Rights Specialist at their Missoula, Montana office. The role involves preparing Montana DNRC water right permits and change applications, GIS analysis, field work, and agency coordination. Candidates need a bachelor's degree in natural resources or related field and at least one year of experience with Montana DNRC water right permitting.
