The University of Glasgow is recruiting a PhD student under supervisors Dr Mu Niu, Dr Craig Wilkie, Prof Cathy Yi-Hsuan Chen, and Dr Michael Tso to develop mathematical and computational methods combining diffusion-based generative AI, Gaussian processes, and Bayesian optimisation for water-quality monitoring across complex constrained domains and networks. The project addresses sparse sensor data and non-Euclidean water system geometries to reconstruct pollution fields and optimise sensor placement. Candidates should have a strong background in mathematics, statistics, or computational science with interest in machine learning and environmental applications.
