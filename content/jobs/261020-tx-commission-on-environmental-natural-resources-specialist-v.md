TCEQ's Office of Water is hiring a senior Natural Resources Specialist to conduct advanced reviews of Texas water rights permit applications, including Water Conservation and Drought Contingency Plans and Regional and State Water Plans. The role requires a bachelor's degree in a natural or physical science, engineering, or related field plus six years of environmental experience (or equivalent advanced degree with fewer years). Key qualifications include experience with Texas surface water rights statutes, water conservation plans, and technical or public communication.
