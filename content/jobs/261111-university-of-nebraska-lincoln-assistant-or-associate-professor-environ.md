The School of Natural Resources at the University of Nebraska-Lincoln is hiring a tenure-track assistant or associate professor for a 9-month appointment split 50% research and 50% teaching. The role focuses on contaminant fate and transport in agricultural landscapes, including nutrient and pesticide legacies and emerging contaminants. A Ph.D. in environmental science, water science, environmental chemistry, or a related field is required, along with demonstrated ability to publish peer-reviewed research and training in environmental chemistry.
