CUAHSI is hiring two part-time reviewers for HydroLearn, an open-source platform for interactive hydrology and water resources learning content. Remote, 5 to 35 hours a week through March 30, 2027, with potential for extension, at $15 to $20 an hour; open only to applicants in the 14 states listed. Apply by November 30, 2026.
