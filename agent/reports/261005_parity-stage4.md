## 1. Rules (how a confidence was assigned)

- **exact**: an identity key agrees: a staff name equal to a name in `full-team.json`; a news file whose `source_url` is the legacy page (6 of 12 news files have one, and all 6 name a page in the inventory); a YouTube id equal to a `youtube_id` (0 matches, see section 4); an apply link equal to a job `url`; a title that normalises equal with the date within 3 days; a "CUAHSI e-Newsletter Month Year" title for that month's issue; a program name equal; the home page. **A judgment (a nav label or function) is never exact** (reviewer finding: I had labelled five such rows exact; the cap is now in the script).
- **probable**: title similarity (shared words over all words, stop words removed) of at least 0.6 with the dates inside a window (30 days for events and jobs, 60 for news, 14 for seminars); or an explicit judgment from the navigation comparison (stage 3, table 1), labelled `nav label`, `nav judgment` or `function`; or the target of a redirect.
- **weak**: similarity of at least 0.4 inside one window, or of at least 0.5 inside three windows; or the same day with similarity of at least 0.3 (a reworded title); or a close title with no legacy date; or a tool named on a landing page.
- **none**: nothing above the thresholds. The notes give the best candidate and its score. The same title with dates more than three windows apart is a **recurrence** (an annual meeting), not a match, and is `none`.
- Jobs: parity.md says "by external URL, then organisation and title"; the script uses the URL, then title and posted date, and **does not use the organisation**. A legacy seminar with no seminar file is also tried against the events here (announced as an event); such a match is at most probable.
- A seminar file with `published: false` is still the counterpart (the legacy page exists) but has **no page here**: the row has an empty `new_route`.
- The thresholds are my choice **(mine)**; they are stated so Jordan can change them.
